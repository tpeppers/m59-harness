Party

Every character in the fleet, side by side. Pick one to plan its schools; similar builds sit together so you can set one and copy it across.
How the numbers on this page are worked out

Learn points. Every character has one budget across seven tracks: the six schools plus Weaponcraft, which pools every weapon skill. A track costs points for the highest level held in it, 1 / 2 / 4 / 6 / 8 / 10 for levels 1–6 (vlLevelPoints, kod/util/system.kod:414). Everything below that level is free. Blink does not count. The budget is 16 + floor(14 × intellect / 5) / 7, rounded down, which is 32 at intellect 40 and 36 at 50 — the most PlayerCanLearn will ever allow, because past it the requirement exceeds three spells at 99%. Base intellect only; an intellect charm buys nothing here.

Ready to learn. The first ability at a new level needs the best three abilities you hold one level below, in the same track, to add up to the requirement: 7 × points-after + 185 − floor(14 × intellect / 5), never below 75. When the level below has only one or two abilities in the whole game, the requirement is divided by 3 or multiplied by ⅔. Once you hold one ability at level 3 or above in a track, the rest of that level is free. Constants: POINTS_SLOPE kod/object/active/holder/nomoveon/battler/player.kod:93, MIN_NEEDED_TO_ADVANCE kod/object/active/holder/nomoveon/battler/player.kod:97, MAX_LEARN_POINTS kod/util/settings.kod:71.

Not modelled, and worth knowing. Level-3 spells in Kraanan, Faren, Shal’ille and Qor need that school's disciple quest, once. Shal’ille needs karma ≥ +10 per level and Qor ≤ −10 per level, so one character cannot plan both. Every point spent outside a school slows improvement inside it, one-for-one.

Saving writes substrate/loadouts/<character>.json — only its plan, through /_plan. Everything else in the file is read from disk at that moment and written back as it was, notes included. The keeper reads it on its next pass: the plan becomes the order it buys in. Gear and the carry list are edited in the loadout planner.

Every number on this site is compiled from the Meridian 59 server source in kod/ and the client sprites in run/localclient/resource. Citations are file:line in that tree. Sprites are the game's own bitmaps, decoded from .bgf. Where a page states a formula, it is the formula the server actually runs — not a community estimate.
