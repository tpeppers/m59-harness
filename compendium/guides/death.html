Death, the Underworld and PvP

Dying in Meridian 59 drops your whole inventory on a corpse, dumps you in the Underworld, and — for anyone past newbie status — costs you exactly one point of maximum health plus a scattering of spell and skill percents. The penalties are not applied at the moment you die; they are applied the moment you leave the Underworld, which is the window in which a Portal of Life can buy them down. Attacking a peaceful player makes you an outlaw; killing one makes you a murderer, a flag that no amount of dying will ever remove.

The death sequence, step by step

Everything below happens inside Player:Killed, in this order.

kod/object/active/holder/nomoveon/battler/player.kod:7908–8201

  1. Death cost is read from server settings. piDeathCost is reset to GetDefaultDeathCost, normally 100. (line 7919; kod/util/settings.kod:59)
  2. Double-death guard. If GetTime() < piLastDeathTime + 2 (seconds), the whole message aborts. You cannot die twice within 2 seconds. (lines 7922–7927)
  3. Any rescue in progress is cancelled. (line 7930)
  4. Cheap escapes. If you are logged on and any of these hold, you do not die at all — your health is set to 1 and the message returns:
    • you are in an arena, in play, and the arena does not have real death on;
    • the room is the OutOfGrace prison;
    • the room answers SafePlayerAttack (i.e. has ROOM_SAFE_DEATH).
    (lines 7965–7984; kod/object/active/holder/room.kod:1124–1131)
  5. All enchantments are stripped — every buff, silently. (line 7989)
  6. "No-drop" death test. You keep everything and take no penalties (piDeathCost set to 0) if any of:
    • a Frenzy (Chaos Night) is running;
    • the room number is in RID_NEWB_BASE..RID_NEWB_MAX (1010–1018);
    • your honor string is still the newbie string;
    • you are wielding a Token (checked slightly later, so a token death still costs you your Necromancer Amulet or Hunter Sword).
    (lines 7996–8035; kod/include/blakston.khd:752, 767)
  7. The corpse is created and placed at your exact tile with fine offsets 16/16. (lines 8038–8039)
  8. Inventory falls onto the corpse. Your natural healing timer is deleted, then every item in plActive and plPassive that the room will accept and whose DropOnDeath returns TRUE is dropped where you fell. If your killer was a player, each dropped item gets the IA_PKPOINTER attribute for 10 minutes. (lines 8044–8079)
  9. Learning progress is gutted.
    piAdvancement_points = 0 piGain_chance = piGain_chance / 2
    plus ResetGainFlags, ResetAtrophyFlags, and the advancement timer is deleted. piGain_chance is your accumulated progress toward the next point of maximum health, so death halves it. (lines 8082–8090; high-water mark formula at 7896–7903)
  10. PK status is re-evaluated (posted), and any Soldier Shield is told you died. (lines 8092–8099)
  11. Consolation prize. If piKarma > 5000 and your home room is not RID_NEWB1, you are handed a Hammer; otherwise if piKarma >= 0 you get a Mace. Both are stamped with the IA_MADE attribute for 18 000 000 ms (5 hours) so they cannot be sold. Negative-karma characters get nothing. (lines 8102–8124)
  12. Dying scream, then UserGotoDeadRoom. (lines 8127–8144)
  13. Health, mana and vigor are set.
    Frenzy: health = max_health / 2 ; mana = max_mana / 2 ; vigor = 100 Otherwise: health = 1 ; mana = 1 ; vigor = bound(vigor/4, 0, 50)
    (lines 8147–8164)
  14. Five seconds of red pain effect (EFFECT_PAIN, duration 5000 ms). (line 8178)
  15. Newbie consolation mail. If piDeathCost > 0 and you are not flagged PFLAG_TUTORIAL and not a murderer, you get a guardian-angel gmail and your mana is set to (max_mana / 2) + 2 so you can broadcast for help. (lines 8181–8193)

PFLAG_TUTORIAL reads backwards from its name. It is set to TRUE the first time you become PK-enabled, or once your character is 2 game years old — i.e. TRUE means "no longer a newbie". So "NOT PFLAG_TUTORIAL" in the death code means "still a newbie".

kod/object/active/holder/nomoveon/battler/player.kod:1543–1545, 11089–11091

Corpses and looting

  • Your corpse is a DeadBody carrying your name, and it decomposes after 10 minutes. (kod/object/passive/body.kod:94)
  • The corpse registers itself with the Underworld's corpse list at creation, and de-registers on decay. (kod/object/passive/body.kod:95; kod/object/active/holder/room/monsroom/uworld.kod:598, 612)
  • If your killer was a User, every item you dropped carries IA_PKPOINTER for 10 minutes. During that window only the dead player themselves or another PFLAG_PKILL_ENABLE character may pick the loot up — non-PK "angeled" characters are "mystically barred". The bar also applies to being offered the item. (kod/object/active/holder/nomoveon/battler/player.kod:8057, 8072; kod/object/passive/itematt/iaPKptr.kod:58–89)
  • Corpses of players killed in Ko'catan are flagged, which changes where the Underworld exit sends you (see below). (kod/object/active/holder/room/monsroom/uworld.kod:598–608)

DeadBody also runs a 25-second ptNoSteal timer with a CanGetMe check. That check is only ever consulted by IA_CORPSEPOINTER, which is attached to loot generated from monster corpses, not to items a player drops. For player deaths the relevant lock is the 10-minute IA_PKPOINTER above.

kod/object/passive/body.kod:100–124; kod/object/passive/itematt/iacorptr.kod:58–72; kod/object/item.kod:114–126

The brazier puzzle

Five braziers, each wired to itself plus two others. On reset, one brazier is unlit, and with probability 2/3 (random(0,2) <> 0) a second one is too. Lighting a brazier toggles its own triple. Get all five lit and the room fires a lightning projectile at the skull and spawns the CorpseNode at row 16, col 16 for DELETE_PORTAL_DELAY = 60 seconds. The puzzle resets when the room is empty on the 10-minute maintenance tick.

kod/object/active/holder/room/monsroom/uworld.kod:17–18, 241–300, 389–434, 460–520

Touching the corpse node while standing within 3 squares also melds it as a mana node, permanently raising your maximum mana by ((5 + Mysticism) / 10) + 3.

kod/object/active/portal/corpnode.kod:17, 133–155, 184–187

Death penalties — applied when you leave, not when you die

The Underworld's LeaveHold removes your corpse from its list and calls ApplyDeathPenalties on you. Until you step through a portal, nothing has been taken yet.

kod/object/active/holder/room/monsroom/uworld.kod:314–333; kod/object/active/holder/nomoveon/battler/player.kod:8203–8324

1. Outlaw and revenant clearing

if piDeathCost >= GetDefaultDeathCost % i.e. >= 100 by default { PFLAG_OUTLAW = FALSE PFLAG_HAUNTED = FALSE % "The revenant is satisfied." }

kod/object/active/holder/nomoveon/battler/player.kod:8219–8234

Only a full-price death launders the outlaw flag. A Frenzy death, a newbie-area death, a token death, an arena death — and any death cheapened by a Portal of Life, since the spell bounds the cost to at most 80 — leaves you an outlaw and leaves your revenant hunting you.

2. Maximum health

if piDeathCost > 0 { if NOT PFLAG_TUTORIAL AND NOT PFLAG_MURDERER % still a newbie, not a murderer piDeathCost = piDeathCost / 3 % no HP roll at all else if random(1,100) <= piDeathCost % lose one base max HP GainBaseMaxHealth(-1) }

kod/object/active/holder/nomoveon/battler/player.kod:8237–8253

At the default death cost of 100, random(1,100) <= 100 is always true: every ordinary death costs an established character exactly one point of base maximum health. GainBaseMaxHealth floors this at 20, so you can never be ground below 20 base HP by dying.

kod/object/active/holder/nomoveon/battler/player.kod:5903–5905

Immediately afterwards, if your base max health has fallen below PKILL_ENABLE_HP (30) you are ejected from your guild.

kod/object/active/holder/nomoveon/battler/player.kod:8270–8273

3. Spell and skill proficiency

iAmount = -2 if PFLAG_MURDERER else -1 for each spell in plSpells: if ability > 5 AND random(1,100) > Stamina AND random(1,100) < piDeathCost then ability += iAmount % clamped to 1..99 for each skill in plSkills: (identical test)

kod/object/active/holder/nomoveon/battler/player.kod:8279–8308; clamp at 6766

Worked example

An established fighter with Stamina 40 and 22 spells, dying normally (cost 100, not a murderer):

  • Base max health: −1, guaranteed.
  • Per spell above 5%: P(random(1,100) > 40) = 60/100, times P(random(1,100) < 100) = 99/100 → 59.4% chance to lose 1%. Expected loss over 22 spells ≈ 13 percentage points.
  • A murderer in the same spot loses 2% per triggered spell — expected ≈ 26 points — and still takes the guaranteed HP loss.
  • A newbie (never PK-enabled, under 2 game years) instead has the cost divided by three: 100/3 = 33, so P(random(1,100) < 33) = 32/100, giving 60/100 × 32/100 = 19.2% per spell, and no health loss at all.

Resurrection: Portal of Life

Portal of Life (Shal'ille 6, 30 mana, 10 s cast, 2 emeralds + 1 polished seraphym) is cast on a corpse, not a person. It creates a CorpsePortal in the Underworld that only that corpse's owner can use, and it reduces the dead player's piDeathCost.

Who may attack whom

AllowPlayerAttack is the gate every player attack passes through, in this exact order:

kod/object/active/holder/nomoveon/battler/player.kod:3615–3765

  1. The room's ReqSomethingAttack must permit it (room flags — see below).
  2. If the room IsArena → allowed, unconditionally.
  3. If a Frenzy (GetChaosNight) is running → allowed.
  4. Mortal event characters are always attackable.
  5. On a SERVER_NO_MURDER ("Sacred Haven") server, IsPKAllowed is FALSE and no player-vs-player attack is permitted at all; even pets are protected. (kod/util/system.kod:5300–5308)
  6. Unless the room has ROOM_KILL_ZONE, both parties must have PFLAG_PKILL_ENABLE. Attacking someone without it gets the "guardian angel" refusal; lacking it yourself gets "you can't hit a newbie".
  7. Finally CheckStatusAndSafety and CheckFactionAttack.

PFLAG_PKILL_ENABLE is granted automatically by EvaluatePKStatus when you join a guild, reach 30 base max health, or become an outlaw or murderer; and revoked when you are unguilded, under 30 base max health, and neither outlaw nor murderer. Becoming killable also permanently sets PFLAG_TUTORIAL and clears your newbie honour string.

kod/object/active/holder/nomoveon/battler/player.kod:11047–11107

Free-fire relationships

CheckStatusAndSafety lets these pairs fight with no outlaw flag and no faction loss:

  • Two guilds that are mutual enemies (IsMutualEnemy).
  • Two Soldier Shield holders on opposing factions (IsEnemyAttack).
  • Anyone holding a Token — a free target.
  • Any target already flagged murderer or outlaw.
  • Targets who are PFLAG_ANONYMOUS or PFLAG_MORPHED.
  • Yourself.

kod/object/active/holder/nomoveon/battler/player.kod:3773–3813, 3789–3795

Safety

PFLAG_SAFETY is a client-toggled interlock. With safety on, an attack on a non-outlaw, non-murderer player simply fails ("safety caught") rather than flagging you. On a Sacred Haven server CheckPlayerFlag reports safety as permanently on.

kod/object/active/holder/nomoveon/battler/player.kod:3819–3838, 1131–1133

The damage cap, and why outlaws die faster

Inside Player:AssessDamage, after defence modifiers, resistances and attack-modifier bonuses, and after a floor of 1 damage:

if piHealth < (piBase_Max_Health * 2) AND ( (NOT PFLAG_OUTLAW AND NOT PFLAG_MURDERER) OR DamageCapProtectionMurderersEnabled ) { iLimit = (piBase_Max_Health + 2) / 3 % MAX_HEALTH_DAMAGE_FRACTION = 3 damage = bound(damage, $, iLimit) } damage = bound(damage, $, 30) % MAX_DAMAGE_PER_HIT % faction enemies, above all caps: if attacker wears a SoldierShield and IsEnemyAttack damage = (damage * 115) / 100

Revenants

Murder can summon a revenant: an invisible, wall-walking, single-minded monster that hunts only the killer.

Guards, bounties and the Justicar

There is no bounty system and no guard that attacks criminals in this codebase. The complete set of consumers of PFLAG_MURDERER/PFLAG_OUTLAW outside player.kod is: the Barloque clerk (Justicar), shopkeeper service checks in monster.kod, two trainers (JealousGeneral, RebelLiege), the guild-hall conquest check, the room's guild-PK gate, the logoff-ghost penalty, the Hunter Sword, a quest-node prize, the Reflection and Evil Twin summons, and a DM spell. Social and economic pressure is the entire enforcement mechanism.

Social consequences of being a murderer

  • Any NPC with MOB_LAWFUL (0x40000) refuses to trade with you if more than one player is in the room; alone with the shopkeeper, you get "illicit service" and the trade goes through. A faction ruling from the Parliament can waive the refusal entirely. (kod/object/active/holder/nomoveon/battler/monster.kod:5062–5097; kod/include/blakston.khd:1407)
  • The JealousGeneral and the RebelLiege refuse to teach you any spell. (kod/object/active/holder/nomoveon/battler/monster/towns/wanderer/jGeneral.kod:147–155; .../factions/rebel.kod:158–166)
  • Picking up a HunterSword as an outlaw or murderer damages you every tick instead of arming you: random(1,2), plus -8 × karma / 100 if your karma is negative. (kod/object/item/passitem/weapon/huntsw.kod:222–243)
  • Outlaws and murderers may not vote for, or stand as, Justicar. (bqclerk.kod:1184–1208)
  • Your death is announced world-wide with a special line — "### %q, an outlaw, …" / "### %q, a murderer, …" — and killing an innocent as a murderer produces the anonymous "### %q has been murdered in cold blood." (kod/util/system.kod:49–58, 1533–1546, 1614–1624)
  • Outlaws and murderers cannot generate revenants when they are the victim. (player.kod:5040–5045)

Where those flags actually appear

ROOM_SAFE_DEATH — exactly two rooms: TosArena (kod/object/active/holder/room/tosrm/tosarena.kod:99) and NecArena (kod/object/active/holder/room/necarena.kod:68). Both also return TRUE from SafePlayerAttack, and both expose ArenaRealDeath so an admin can switch real death on (tosarena.kod:748–752).

ROOM_GUILD_PK_ONLY — the open streets of every town, so unguilded non-criminals cannot be jumped in the street: Tos, EastTos, TosArena2, BarloqueNorth, BarloqueSouth, BarloquePort, CorNoth, Jasper, JasperWest, JasperAB1, Kocatan, KocatanSouth, Castle1, Castle1B, H6. (barln.kod:39, barls.kod:30, barlport.kod:28, cornoth.kod:30, jasper.kod:30, jaswest.kod:32, jasab1.kod:29, kocatan.kod:29, kocsouth.kod:40, tos.kod:31, easttos.kod:29, tosaren2.kod:33, castle1.kod, castle1b.kod, h6.kod)

ROOM_NO_COMBAT — every shop, bank, tavern, hall, guest room, the Barloque court and jail, the University, both Trading Posts, and the Underworld itself.

ROOM_SAFELOGOFF — the five town inns (Bar, Cor, Jasper, Marion, Tos), the Ko'catan Inn, the adventurers' halls (Bar, Cor, Jasper, Marion, Tos, Ko'catan House of Healing), the Jasper and Ko'catan taverns, the Gallery, the University, Field1, the Trading Post, rented rooms, the Out of Grace prison and the Underworld. Note that the base GuildHall class has ROOM_SAFELOGOFF commented out (ghall.kod:124); guild halls get their safe-logoff treatment through the special case in UserLogoff instead.

ROOM_KILL_ZONE and ROOM_NO_PK — no room in the shipped world sets either as a permanent flag. They exist only for runtime SetRoomFlag use.

Two more places behave as sanctuaries without using a flag: OutOfGrace (room 43) hard-refuses every attack and every non-DM spell in overridden messages, and the Underworld is ROOM_NO_COMBAT and refuses assassin-game attacks outright.

Logging off in the field

Quitting outside a ROOM_SAFELOGOFF room (or a guild hall you may enter, outside its foyer) leaves a LogoffGhost standing there. You have a grace period of GetLogoffPenaltyGhostTime = 600 seconds (10 minutes), randomised to 90–110% of that, to reconnect. Reconnect in time and nothing happens.

kod/object/active/holder/nomoveon/battler/player/user.kod:665–690; kod/util/settings.kod:94

penaltyCounter = piLogoffPenaltyCount + piKill_count_decay iEquivDeath = 10 % system.kod:340 itemsToDrop = numItems * penaltyCounter / iEquivDeath (+1 more with probability penaltyCounter/(iEquivDeath-1) when under) maxHealthLost = penaltyCounter / iEquivDeath (+1 more on the remainder roll) spellPointsLost= numSpells * penaltyCounter / iEquivDeath, at 1 per spell (2 per spell if PFLAG_MURDERER), never below 5%

kod/object/active/logghost.kod:263–265, 270–278, 336–342, 356–360; kod/util/system.kod:340

So ten unsafe logoffs (or fewer, if you have unjustified kills on the books) is a full death's worth of loss. IncrementLogoffPenaltyCount adds 2 instead of 1 if you attacked a player in the last 15 minutes — logging out mid-fight is punished double. The counter decays by 1 on a system timer.

kod/object/active/holder/nomoveon/battler/player/user.kod:760–773, 748–752; kod/util/system.kod:1141

Being helped by others

CanHelpPlayer() = (piTimeAttackedPlayer + ATTACKED_PLAYER_WAIT) <= GetTime() OR GetChaosNight() ATTACKED_PLAYER_WAIT = 15 * 60 seconds

kod/object/active/holder/nomoveon/battler/player.kod:3948–3958, 105

For 15 minutes after you attack another player, nobody may cast a helpful spell on you unless the room's guild-PK rules already allow them to fight you. This is the anti-"mule healer" rule: you cannot park an unkillable, angeled character behind the front line to keep the fighters topped up. The clock is set in CheckStatusAndSafety the moment your attack is approved.

kod/object/passive/spell.kod:578–592, 874–884; kod/object/active/holder/nomoveon/battler/player.kod:3810, 3860

Rescue

Rescue (Shal'ille 3, 16 mana, 1 emerald) is the escape hatch, not a resurrection. It cannot be started within piTeleportAttackDelaySec = 10 minutes of your own last player attack, nor while holding a Token, nor while another rescue is pending.

delay = 1000 * 15 % piRescueBaseDelaySec = 15 delay = delay - (SpellPower * delay) / 100 / 4 if random(1,2) = 1: delay = delay + random(5000, 10000)

At spell power 99 that is 15000 − 3712 = 11.3 s, plus a coin-flip 5–10 s. Any attack you make cancels a pending rescue, as does dying. The destination is your guild hall if it is in the same region, else the Ko'catan Inn (in Ko'catan), else the Pool of Vigor (in the orc caves), else your home room.

kod/object/passive/spell/rescue.kod:38–44, 64–90, 92–108, 110–166; kod/util/settings.kod:88, 91; kod/object/active/holder/nomoveon/battler/player.kod:1364–1418, 3756–3762, 7930

Frenzies (Chaos Night)

During a Frenzy every rule above relaxes at once, and this is the single largest behavioural switch in the game:

  • AllowPlayerAttack returns TRUE for anything the room permits — no PK-enable check, no safety, no newbie protection. (player.kod:3646–3649)
  • Deaths are no-drop and cost nothing. (player.kod:7996–8003)
  • You respawn with half health, half mana and full vigor instead of 1/1/quartered. (player.kod:8147–8153)
  • ApplyDeathPenalties returns immediately, after clearing PFLAG_HAUNTED. (player.kod:8207–8214)
  • No revenants are created. (player.kod:4906)
  • No karma changes from player kills. (player.kod:6530–6534)
  • No faction loss. (player.kod:3874–3881)
  • Anyone may be healed regardless of recent attacks. (player.kod:3951–3952)
  • The Underworld ignores Frenzies entirely so bodies do not pile up. (uworld.kod:712–723)
  • ROOM_NO_COMBAT is suppressed on non-hometown rooms while the Frenzy runs. (kod/object/active/holder/room.kod:1167–1184)

Odds and ends worth knowing

  • A Necromancer Amulet wearer can be killed while logged off; the corpse and drops are placed at the saved logoff location instead of the current room. (player.kod:7937–7960, 7948–7952)
  • Guests never enter the Underworld: Guest:UserGotoDeadRoom sends them to RID_GUEST1, and guest deaths are not broadcast. (kod/object/active/holder/nomoveon/battler/player/user/guest.kod:63–68; kod/util/system.kod:1461–1466)
  • Dying inside the newbie area (rooms 1010–1018) teleports you to RID_NEWB1 rather than the Underworld, so ApplyDeathPenalties is never reached. (kod/object/active/holder/nomoveon/battler/player/user.kod:2285–2304)
  • Suicide (UC_SUICIDE) is blocked if you are a guild master, in Out of Grace, the sitting Justicar, holding a Token, or within SUICIDE_REPEAT_TIME = 600 seconds of your last restart. (kod/object/active/holder/nomoveon/battler/player/user.kod:31, 1494–1538)
  • Karma from a player kill uses the strongest swing factor, piKillKarmaSwingPlayer = 10, against piKillKarmaSwingMonster = 6 for monsters, and is skipped entirely in arenas, in the newbie region, and during Frenzies. See Karma. (player.kod:6524–6592; kod/util/settings.kod:51–53)
  • Alternate server presets change the death cost: server 101 uses piDefaultDeathCost = 90, server 102 uses 60. The stock configuration (piServerNumber = 0) uses 100. (kod/util/settings.kod:161–186; kod/util/system.kod:393, 2661)
