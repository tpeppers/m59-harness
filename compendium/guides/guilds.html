Guilds

A guild is a rented institution. It has five ranks, an elected master, a treasury that is charged every six minutes whether anyone is playing or not, and a hall that other guilds can take from you by force. Everything about it costs money continuously, and running out does not shrink the guild — it deletes it.

Electing a master

every member's vote is recorded against a candidate if a guildmaster already holds more than half the members' votes: no election happens otherwise the candidate with the most votes wins, provided that count exceeds half the membership

kod/object/passive/guild.kod:1884 CountVotes

The sitting master needs a majority, not a plurality, to be safe — and a challenger also needs an outright majority to take the seat. A guild split three ways simply keeps whoever is in place, because nobody clears the bar.

Rent, and how guilds die

The maintenance timer charges the guild every period. The bill is assembled from scratch each time:

rent = RENT_BASE % 5 + RENT_SECRET if secret % +10 + RENT_PER_MEMBER × members % +1 each if the guild holds a hall: + hall.GetRentValue() / 10 + RENT_PER_ALLY × allies % −2 each — allies REDUCE the bill + RENT_PER_ENEMY × enemies % +5 each — enemies raise it

kod/object/passive/guild.kod:776 AccrueRent, constants at :38–43

Falling behind deletes the guild. When the overdue counter reaches its limit, every member is messaged and the guild object is deleted outright. There is no grace period beyond RENT_MAX_OVERDUE — 480 six-minute periods, which is 48 hours of arrears. kod/object/passive/guild.kod:786–796, :46

Read the modifiers again, because they are the whole political economy of guilds:

  • Every member costs 1 per period. A 400-member guild pays 400 per period on membership alone. Guilds are not free to grow.
  • Every ally saves you 2 per period. Alliances are worth more than the membership of two people. This is a direct financial subsidy for diplomacy.
  • Every enemy costs 5 per period. Wars are expensive to hold, not just to fight, and the cost accrues while you sleep.
  • Allies and enemies only count if you hold a hall. A landless guild pays no diplomatic premium at all — and gets no alliance discount either.

War

to declare an enemy, the guild's treasury must be able to absorb the penalty: GetRentDue() ≤ −WAR_LOSS_PENALTY % 50,000 in hand if you pull out of a mutually declared war: you lose WAR_LOSS_PENALTY (50,000) the vanquished enemy's conqueror gains: WAR_WINNER_PERCENT of it (60%, i.e. 30,000)

kod/object/passive/guild.kod:2289–2298, constants at :27–29

You cannot declare a war you could not afford to lose. That single check is what stops small guilds harassing large ones for free, and it makes 50,000 shillings the effective entry fee to guild politics.

Ending a war is not instantaneous either — peace runs on a delay of PEACE_DELAY, two hours, during which the declaration is still live.

kod/object/passive/guild.kod:50

Mutual enmity has a combat consequence worth knowing: two players whose guilds are mutually at war may kill each other without either becoming a murderer. The check is explicit in the kill handler. kod/object/active/holder/nomoveon/battler/player.kod:4865–4875

The guild hall

A hall is the guild's property and its liability. It adds a tenth of its own rent value to the bill every period, it switches on the ally and enemy modifiers, and it can be taken:

  • Conquered — another guild takes it by force.
  • Betrayed — the same outcome by a different route, and the game sends a different message so members know which happened.

kod/object/passive/guild.kod:1009 GuildHallConquered

When it falls, every member's guild powers are reset and offline members are sent mail about it. A room may be flagged ROOM_GUILD_PK_ONLY, restricting combat inside it to guild conflict.

kod/include/blakston.khd:1040

Colours and shields

A guild carries a primary colour, a secondary colour and a shield shape, and members display them. This is what makes a guild visible in the world rather than merely recorded.

Founding one

Guilds are chartered by an NPC with the occupation MOB_ROLE_GUILD_CREATOR, who lives in Barloque. You need three members to hold a guild together at all.

kod/object/active/holder/nomoveon/battler/monster/towns/barlqtwn/gcreator.kod:321 · kod/include/blakston.khd:1425

Practical advice

  • Ally early. Each alliance pays for two members' worth of rent, permanently, and costs nothing but the diplomacy.
  • Do not hold a hall you cannot fund. The hall is what switches on the enemy premium; a landless guild at war pays nothing extra.
  • Watch the treasury before declaring anything. 50,000 is the price of admission, and pulling out costs all of it.
  • Secrecy costs triple base rent and doubles the maturity wait. It is a real expense, not a flag.
  • Prune the roster. Every inactive member is 1 per period forever.
