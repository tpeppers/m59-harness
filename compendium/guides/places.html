Places and Travel

The world is 268 rooms that create creatures, grouped into six settlements and the wilderness between them. What a room lets you do is decided by a bitmask of sixteen flags — one of which turns off death, one of which turns off magic, and one of which makes anyone in it killable. Reading those flags before you walk in is the difference between a safe town and a kill zone that looks like one.

The sixteen room flags

Every one of these is checked somewhere in the server. The descriptions are the game's own comments.

ROOM_SAFE_DEATH also switches off progression. AdvancementCheck returns immediately in such a room, and so does ImproveAbility. An arena without real death is a place where nothing you do counts — neither the dying nor the winning. kod/object/active/holder/nomoveon/battler/player.kod:7748–7753 · kod/object/passive/spell.kod:1641–1646
Two flags do the same job at different strengths: ROOM_SANCTUARY doubles vigour recovery and ROOM_TRIPLE_HEAL triples it. If you are resting between fights, resting in the right room is worth a third of the time.

The Underworld

RID_UNDERWORLD is region 1. It is where you wake after dying, and it has no ordinary exits — see Death, the Underworld and PvP for how to leave and what it costs.

kod/include/blakston.khd:375

The newbie region

RID_NEWB_BASE is exempted from karma changes entirely — killing anything there moves your alignment not at all. The stated reason is protective: without it, a new character could accidentally push their karma past the gate on a spell they already own and render it uncastable.

kod/object/active/holder/nomoveon/battler/player.kod:6536–6542

Arenas

An arena is checked separately from the room flags, and two things follow from it: no karma changes, and no advancement unless real death is switched on. An arena is for practice and for spectacle, not for progress.

kod/object/active/holder/nomoveon/battler/player.kod:6544–6548, :7750

Where the room changes your magic

Three of the six schools read the room itself when computing spell power, so where you stand is worth up to 30 points on a 99-point scale:

SchoolWhat it reads
Shal'illeThe room's natural setting — GetShalilleBonus
FarenThe room's water and fire — GetFarenBonus
KraananThe number of active beings in the room

kod/object/passive/spell.kod:2107–2184

And ROOM_ANTI_MAGIC subtracts the antimagic aura's value from spell power directly, partly offset by any mana convergence in effect.

Getting around

  • Blink — Riija level 1, 15 mana, teleports you to the local place of power. A ten-second trance, so it is a travel spell, not an escape.
  • Elusion — Riija level 6, instant. This is the escape.
  • Portal of Life — Shal'ille.
  • Inns — a room with ROOM_HOMETOWN can be declared your home, which is where several effects return you to.
  • Safe logoff — ROOM_SAFELOGOFF rooms cost you nothing for leaving. Elsewhere, logging off has a penalty.

Where a new character should go

  1. The newbie region first. Karma cannot be damaged there, and while your maximum health is under 30 every kill carries a bonus advancement point.
  2. Then a town's outskirts — Barloque's sewers, the streets of Tos. Low-level creatures, a bank and an Adventurer's Hall within reach.
  3. Then the wilderness by difficulty, not level. Sort the bestiary by how likely a creature is to hit you and work down. Fighting things above your level is the only thing that raises maximum health.
  4. The jungle and the Fey Forest late. The Avar camps and Elhadrim hold the high-difficulty creatures — and the best treasure tables, because drop count keys off difficulty.
