Riija — the School of Illusion

Riija is the Trickster, and the school named for him is the only one in the game built around not being where you appear to be. Twenty-three spells: teleports, disguises, false walls, false wounds, false selves. It has no karma requirement, its power waxes and wanes with the moon, and — uniquely — it rewards you for carrying more rather than less.

Intellect is the best requisite attribute in the game to be saddled with, because it pays three times over: it sets the floor under Riija's success chance, it adds up to 50% to the first learning gate for everything you learn, and it damps atrophy across your whole ability list. A Riija caster's core attribute is quietly working for their fighting skills too.

The two things that make Riija strange

primary bonus (0–30) = the moon's overlay height in the sky / 12 secondary bonus (0–10) = bulk_held × 10 / bulk_max

kod/object/passive/spell.kod:2146–2160

The moon. Riija's world-state bonus is read off the position of the moon among the background objects — the source notes its range as −16 to 30 before bounding. No other school's power varies on a cycle the player cannot influence at all. A Riija caster is measurably stronger at some hours than others, and there is nothing to be done about it but notice.

The pack. Every other school treats encumbrance as pure cost. Riija turns it into up to 10 points of spell power: the fuller your bulk, the stronger you cast. A Riija caster who empties their pack to move quickly is throwing away a tenth of the scale. This is the only place in the game where the correct answer is carry more.

Both bonuses are added to half your ability in the spell, so at low ability they dominate. A new Riija caster standing under a high moon with a full pack casts meaningfully better than the same character empty-handed at moonset. See Spells and Spellcasting for the whole spell-power calculation.

What the school is actually for

Not being there

Blink, elusion and feign death are three different answers to the same question. Blink is a teleport but costs a ten-second trance — an eternity in a fight, and only survivable if you start it before you need it. Elusion at level 6 is instant. Feign death costs 5 mana and no cast time at all, and is the cheapest exit in the school.

Not being who you are

Anonymity, shadow form, illusionary form and morph form a ladder of concealment. Morph is the real prize and the real commitment: a twenty-second trance, and while morphed your offence, defence, damage, parry, block and dodge are all taken from the creature you have become rather than from you.

kod/object/active/holder/nomoveon/battler/player.kod:4232, :4300, :4322, :4416 — every combat accessor checks PFLAG_MORPHED first

Morphing has an advancement penalty attached. If your maximum health is more than double its base and you kill something weaker than that, your health gain is halved and the roll is skipped entirely. The game explicitly calls this "cheesing the situation." kod/object/active/holder/nomoveon/battler/player.kod:7809–7816

Not being alone

Reflection, apparition and evil twin put copies of you on the board.

You cannot advance on anything you summon. Illusions and revenants both refuse — CanPlayerAdvanceOnMe returns false — and summoned creatures drop no treasure at all. Summons are for winning fights, never for farming them. kod/object/active/holder/nomoveon/battler/monster.kod:4944 · kod/object/passive/spell.kod:1624

Ruining someone else's day

Dement is the school's quiet weapon: it subtracts random(n, 2n) directly from the target's spell power, which cuts their success chance, raises their mana costs and slows their casting all at once. Vertigo, flash and forget attack perception and memory; seduce at level 6 attacks loyalty.

kod/object/passive/spell.kod:2229–2236 — dementia is applied inside GetSpellPower itself

Playing it

  • Cast under a high moon. Up to 30 of the 99-point spell power scale is the moon's position. Nothing else you do moves the number that far.
  • Stay loaded. Bulk is spell power here. Empty your pack only when you have to.
  • Level 1 is unusually strong. Feign death and shadow form both cost 5 mana, and blink is a free teleport home. A level-1 Riija caster has a full escape kit.
  • Watch the trances. Blink is 10 seconds, morph is 20, artifice is 10. Those are pre-fight spells. Everything you want mid-fight in this school — flash, vertigo, dement, elusion, feign death — is instant, and that split is not an accident.
  • Firesand, rainbow fern, solagh, web moss, uncut seraphym and dragonfly eye between them cover almost the whole school. See Reagents for where they drop and what they cost.
A piece of lore that is also a mechanical hint: the game says Duke Akardius is "a zealous devotee of Riija," and his description points at "the manipulative, deceitful aspect of the Trickster God." The school and the political layer are written as the same theme. kod/object/active/holder/nomoveon/battler/monster/factions/duke.kod:22
