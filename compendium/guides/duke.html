The Duke and Politics

Above the fighting there is a second game, played by three courts, scored continuously, and worth real combat statistics to everyone who joins in. Parliament recomputes the standings every twenty minutes from two contests — who holds the territory flags, and who holds the loyalty of the Senate — and hands the winners measurable bonuses to hit, defence, damage, spell power, regeneration and vigour.

How power is scored

Parliament sums the points of every running faction game, for each faction:

for each faction: count = Σ game.GetPoints(faction) over every faction game raw power(faction) = count standing = FigurePowerFromCount(count) total_power_points = Σ game.GetTotalPoints() balance = total_power_points × balance_factor / 100

kod/util/parlia.kod:417 RedoTotalPower, :434 RedoPower

Game one: territory

Faction flags are planted and counted; a faction's share of the total flags is its share of the territory points. Every flag on the board contributes to somebody.

kod/util/factgame/territry.kod:848 GetPoints

Game two: the Senate

Each councillor in the Senate is loyal to one faction and contributes exactly one point to it. Tokens delivered to a councillor set and hold that loyalty — and the source is explicit that giving a councillor extra tokens buys stability, not score:

Giving counselors extra tokens keeps them from swinging to other factions too easily, but doesn't increase value.

kod/util/factgame/tokngame.kod:962–980

That is a deliberate anti-hoarding rule. Spreading tokens across many councillors is worth strictly more than piling them onto one, and the optimal play is breadth.

Delivering a token to the Duke gets you a distinctive response, quoted directly from the game: "I will see that this gets returned to its rightful councilor. Of course, he will owe me." The political game is explicitly a game of debts. kod/object/active/holder/nomoveon/battler/monster/factions/duke.kod:31

What a faction's standing buys its members

balance = total_power_points × balance_factor / 100 power contribution: power ≤ balance: p = (110 × power / balance) − 10 power > balance: p = 100 + 50 × (power − balance) / balance p = bound(p, −10, 150) membership contribution: m = 100 if members ≤ balance, or balance < 3 = −50 × members / mbalance + 150 otherwise m = bound(m, 25, 100) bonus = base × p × m / 10000 if p > 0 = base × p / 100 if p ≤ 0

The full treatment of what this means at the table is on Factions. The short version: a faction above balance makes its members roughly four times better off than neutrality on six statistics at once, and a faction below balance makes them worse off than neutrality — which cannot go negative at all.

The asymmetry is the point. The standings move on a twenty-minute clock; you move on a twenty-eight-hour one. You cannot chase the bonus, only bet on it.

Joining, and being turned away

Only intriguing players participate: player-killing enabled, and either advanced enough in health or adept enough in ability. Everyone else collects the flat unaligned bonus and is untouched by any of this.

kod/object/active/holder/nomoveon/battler/player.kod:11316 PlayerIsIntriguing

A liege will refuse you if their faction is already too strong — the Duke says so in as many words: "If we were not already a very powerful faction, I would offer to let you join us." The system actively resists snowballing, in the recruiting as well as in the membership dilution term of the bonus formula.

kod/object/active/holder/nomoveon/battler/monster/factions/duke.kod:35

Politics and the law

  • Enemy soldiers do 15% more damage to each other, applied after both damage caps — the only thing in the game that breaks the 30-per-hit ceiling. kod/object/active/holder/nomoveon/battler/player.kod:4620
  • Killing an enemy soldier is not murder. The murderer check is skipped when your soldier shield says the target was a legitimate enemy. kod/object/active/holder/nomoveon/battler/player.kod:4870
  • Faction pressure can override a lawful merchant's refusal to trade with a murderer. kod/util/parlia.kod:1468
  • Parliament can be shut down entirely, at which point everyone stops being intriguing and the whole layer goes dormant. kod/util/parlia.kod:399 Shutdown
