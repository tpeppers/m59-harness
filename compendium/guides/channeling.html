Channeling and Mana

Mana is the only currency your spells spend. Your maximum is set by Mysticism plus every mana node you have ever melded with; the refill rate is set by a single formula whose two big levers are Vigor and that same maximum. There is no cap-restoring potion, no mana-for-health trade except one helmet, and no skill called "channeling" — the fast regen everyone talks about is the ManaFocus spell sitting on top of an ordinary timer.

"Channeling" is not a mechanic in this game. A case-insensitive search of kod/, clientd3d/ and blakserv/ finds the word only in three places: NPC and item flavor text (kod/object/active/holder/room/hermhut.kod:29, kod/object/active/holder/room/monsroom/g9.kod:88, kod/object/passive/spell/persench/touchatk/zap.kod:22, kod/object/item/actitem/necroam.kod:52), a chat-etiquette message (kod/object/active/holder/room/guest5/newb5.kod:32), and the server's unrelated logging subsystem (blakserv/channel.c, the [Channel] config section). There is no Channeling skill, spell, or class. When players say "channeling" they mean the mana economy documented on this page — melding nodes, tranced mana regeneration under Mana Focus, and bard-sung Rejuvenate.

Maximum mana

Max mana is stored in piMax_Mana and is fully recomputed by ComputeMaxMana from four sources.

piMax_mana = GetInitialMaxMana() + SUM over melded nodes of node.GetManaAdjust(you) + SUM over items in use with AdjustsMaxMana of item.GetManaChange() + SUM over enchantments with AffectsMaxMana of ench.GetManaBonus(state)

kod/object/active/holder/nomoveon/battler/player.kod:6115

The base: Mysticism only

GetInitialMaxMana() = 15 + Mysticism/5 % integer division GetMysticism() = bound(piMysticism + piMysticismMod, 1, 70)

kod/object/active/holder/nomoveon/battler/player.kod:5965 and :6394 (MAXIMUM_STAT = 70 at :116)

Intellect does not appear anywhere in the mana system. It gates Ri'ija and Jala spell success (kod/object/passive/spell.kod:409) but contributes nothing to your pool or your regen.

The comment block at kod/object/active/holder/nomoveon/battler/player.kod:33 says the base regeneration time is tuned for "a player of intelligence 25 to recover mana." That comment is stale. The code at :5656 reads GetMysticism, not GetIntellect. Build a caster on Mysticism.

Your base Mysticism (piMysticism) is set once, at character creation, from the six stat values you distribute (each 1–50, sum ≤ 200), and is never raised again by play — grep the file: the only assignments are at character creation (kod/object/active/holder/nomoveon/battler/player.kod:2086 and :2095). Everything later moves piMysticismMod instead, via AddMysticism, which itself calls ComputeMaxMana (:9964).

Regeneration

Mana ticks on a self-rescheduling timer. Every tick you gain exactly 1 point if you are below max, or lose exactly 1 point if you are above it.

ManaTimer(): if piMana < piMax_mana: GainMana(1) else: LoseMana(1)

kod/object/active/holder/nomoveon/battler/player.kod:2664

The timer exists only while piMana <> piMax_mana; at exactly full it is deleted (player.kod:6226–6235). Unlike health regen, mana regen does not require you to have moved since entering the room — compare HealthTimer at player.kod:2640, which is gated on PFLAG_MOVED_SINCE_ENTRY. Standing perfectly still regenerates mana at full rate.

The regeneration formula

CalculateManaTime() -> milliseconds until the next mana point if piMana > piMax_mana: % overcharged t = BOOST_DECAY_TIME % 30000 ms; you LOSE 1 mana every 30 s else: t = 150000 + (25 - Mysticism)*1000 % BASE_REGEN_TIME = 150000 t = t * 200 / bound(Vigor, 1, $) t = t / bound(piMax_Mana, 1, $) t = t - FactionRegenBonus(you, hpregen=FALSE) t = bound(t, 1000, 60000) if you are in a room: if a Rejuvenate song is playing here: t = t * (200 - jalaSpellPower) / 200 t = bound(t, 500, 60000) if you are enchanted with Mana Focus: t = bound(t * (200 - focusSpellPower) / 200, 500, 60000)

kod/object/active/holder/nomoveon/battler/player.kod:5645–5693; constants BASE_REGEN_TIME = 150000 at :48 and BOOST_DECAY_TIME = 30000 at :63

Two consequences worth internalising:

  • Dividing by max mana means a bigger pool refills at the same wall-clock speed but delivers far more mana per second. Ignoring the faction subtraction, time to fill from empty is (150000 + (25−Myst)·1000) · 200 / Vigor milliseconds regardless of how large your pool is. Melding nodes therefore multiplies your mana-per-minute roughly linearly.
  • Vigor is a straight divisor. Vigor 200 regenerates exactly twice as fast as Vigor 100.

Vigor: how to raise the divisor

Vigor sits in piVigor, starts at 100, and is hard-capped at viMax_vigor = 200 (kod/object/active/holder/nomoveon/battler/player.kod:829, :740, bounded in NewVigor at :1217).

  • Resting (StartResting, player.kod:9984) burns off exertion every 1000 + 30·(51 − Stamina) ms (:10009), but RestAddExertion clamps vigor at piVigor_rest_threshold, which defaults to 80 and is bounded to 10–100 (:891, :1238, :1296–1334). Resting can never take you above your threshold, and that threshold is 80 unless something has changed it. Inns double the rate (ROOM_SANCTUARY) and triple-heal rooms triple it (:1315–1323). You also cannot cast at all while resting (kod/object/passive/spell.kod:739).
  • Eating is the only route past the threshold. EatSomething calls AddExertion(-10000 · nutrition) (player.kod:5738), i.e. one vigor point per point of nutrition, and that path is not threshold-clamped — only NewVigor's ceiling of 200 stops it. Fullness (piStomach) empties at FOOD_USE_RATE = 12 per 100 ms of elapsed time and blocks eating past 100 (player.kod:51, :5703, :1341).

Because stomach space is the limiting resource, what matters is nutrition per unit of filling. The full treatment — what spends vigor, what the rest threshold is and what moves it — is on Vigor.

Mana Focus and Rejuvenate

Both use the identical multiplier t · (200 − spellpower) / 200. Spell power is bounded to 1–99 (kod/include/blakston.khd:2089–2090), so each one can at best cut the tick time to 101/200 ≈ 50.5 %. Applied together they reach ≈ 25.5 %, but the 500 ms floor usually bites first.

Mana Focus is a trance and dies to almost any interruption (BreakTrance), with one carve-out: casting an AttackSpell does not break it (kod/object/passive/spell/focus.kod:110–118). Movement, being damaged, using an item, changing rooms, resting, and drinking a mana crystal all do (kod/object/active/holder/nomoveon/battler/player/user.kod:2979, :4621, :4676; player.kod:1457, :5767, :9991; kod/object/item/passitem/manacrys.kod:122).

Only one Jala song can be active in a room at a time — GetJalaInfo returns the first Jala enchantment it finds (kod/object/active/holder/room.kod:3473). A bard cannot stack Rejuvenate with Mana Convergence in the same room.

Overcharge decay

Every mana-granting message in the game calls GainMana(amount) with bCapped left at its FALSE default — no caller in the entire tree passes TRUE (kod/object/active/holder/nomoveon/battler/player.kod:6199). So every external source can push you above your maximum. While above it, the same timer bleeds off exactly 1 mana every BOOST_DECAY_TIME = 30 seconds (player.kod:63, :5652).

Mana nodes

A mana node is a PassiveObject you activate (double-click) while standing within range. Melding is permanent, free, instant, and repeatable across the whole node set — there is no upkeep and no limit on how many you hold.

TryActivate(who): requires abs(yourRow - nodeRow) < 3 AND abs(yourCol - nodeCol) < 3 % MANANODE_RANGE = 3 requires the node not be in state NODE_DEAD requires you not already hold this node's bit in piNodelist -> Meld: NewMaxMana(GetManaAdjust(you), node_num) GetManaAdjust(who) = ((5 + who.GetMysticism()) / 10) + 3 % 0 if the node is dead

Nodes that vanish do not take your mana with them. NodeDisappear removes the node from the room but deliberately leaves it in the system list "so that a player's max mana can always be figured on the fly" (kod/object/passive/mananode.kod:138–142). Once melded with the Heart of Zjiria, the Q node or the Avar node, the bonus is yours whether or not the node is currently visible.

Karma-locked nodes

  • Fey node (kod/object/passive/mananode/feynode.kod) gives double the standard adjustment (:135). It appears aligned good or evil. Attempting to meld with the wrong karma triggers LashOut for FEYNODE_DAMAGE = 10 damage, which can kill you (:24, :122–131). Worse, when the node flips alignment it runs StripUsers and unmelds every player of the opposing karma (:72–104).
  • Avar node (kod/object/passive/mananode/avarnode.kod) is also karma-gated but merely refuses you (:57–70) — and, unlike the fey node, it never strips anyone.

Node attacks: your max mana can drop while you are offline

The NodeAttack utility periodically sends Xeochicatl against a node. If the defenders lose, the node is Deactivated — and a dead node's GetManaAdjust returns 0, so every melded player's max mana falls by that node's worth on their next ComputeMaxMana (which fires on login, kod/object/active/holder/nomoveon/battler/player.kod:1885).

Spending mana

Per-cast cost

GetManaCost(who, spellPower): iCost = viMana % per-spell class variable, default 1 if iCost = 0: return 0 if spellPower > 40: iCost = iCost - 1 if spellPower > 80: iCost = iCost - 1 if wielding a Princess shield: iCost = iCost - (iCost * reductionPercent + 99) / 100 % ceiling division return bound(iCost, 1, $)

kod/object/passive/spell.kod:1544–1580; default viMana = 1 at kod/object/passive/spell.kod:161

The Princess shield reduction scales linearly with faction rank from 5 % at rank 1 to 25 % at rank 10 (kod/object/item/passitem/defmod/shield/soldshld/prinshld.kod:24–25, :185). Because the subtraction rounds up, a 10-mana spell at rank 10 costs 7, not 8.

Costs are paid in PayCosts, after a success roll. A spell that fails the roll costs half the mana and half the exertion and no reagents (kod/object/passive/spell.kod:1256–1290, :1150–1170). Immortals pay nothing, and non-Player casters (monsters) pay nothing at all — the message returns TRUE immediately at spell.kod:1262.

Crystalize mana therefore lets a bard carry mana past their cap (up to 130 % of max mana or 250, whichever is lower) but never creates any. Jala 6; 20 mana to start, 1 sapphire and 1 uncut seraphym (crysmana.kod:44–52). Only one crystal per singer exists at a time (crysmana.kod:82–86), and the crystal survives death (DropOnDeath returns FALSE, manacrys.kod:151).

The crystal's colour tells you its charge: one step per 30 stored mana through sky, red, orange, yellow, green, blue, purple (manacrys.kod:58–62, :80). Purple means 180+.

Reading the mana bar

The server sends mana as a numeric stat with a fixed display range of 0–100 and a separate "current max" limit line at your real maximum:

AddPacket(1,2, 4,user_stat_mana, 1,STAT_VALUE, 1,STAT_INTEGER, 4,piMana, 4,0, 4,100, 4,piMax_mana);

kod/object/active/holder/nomoveon/battler/player/user.kod:6663–6669; the client maps these to GRPH_RANGESET / GRPH_POSSET / GRPH_LIMITSET at module/merintr/statnum.c:196–198

So the bar is drawn against a hard 0–100 scale with a marker at your maximum. If you push past 100 mana — easy with several nodes, and trivial with a mana crystal or Defile — the bar pegs and stops being informative. The redraw fires from NewMana, which every gain and loss calls (player.kod:6217–6239).

Quick reference: what actually makes you cast more

  1. Mysticism at creation. It is the only stat in the mana formulas, it sets both the base pool and the per-node bonus, and base Mysticism can never be raised later.
  2. Meld every node you can reach. Each one is a permanent, free, unconditional increase to both your pool and your mana-per-second. The Fey node is worth two.
  3. Eat before you fight. Vigor 200 versus vigor 100 is exactly double regen. Inky caps and chocolate mints are the efficient choices; mints also hand you 5 mana directly.
  4. Mana Focus while you idle. Roughly halves the tick time and survives casting attack spells.
  5. Don't broadcast. At the default 50 % setting a single broadcast costs half your maximum mana.
