Quests

Meridian 59 has a real quest engine, not a collection of hand-written errands. Seventy-five quests are declared as data — a list of nodes, a set of restrictions on who may be offered it, a schedule chance and a cap on how many can run at once — and the engine assigns them to eligible players on its own clock. You do not go looking for quests. They are offered to you, and whether you qualify is decided by your karma, your faction, your guild, your gear and your own history with that quest.

How a quest is put together

A quest template is seven values:

[ number of players, % 1…5 quest type, % competitive / cooperative / collective player restrictions, % a bitmask, see below [ node indices ], % the steps, in order max simultaneously active, % how many copies of this quest may run at once [ restrictions2 ], % parameterised restrictions schedule chance ] % percentage chance the scheduler picks it

kod/util/questengine.kod:1628 onward — every quest in the game is one cons onto plQuestTemplates

And each node is one step, described by twelve fields: the NPCs who can serve as its giver, a type, cargo, monsters, prizes, penalties, three hint strings and a time limit.

kod/util/questengine.kod — QNT_NPC_LIST … QNT_TIME_LIMIT, constants at the head of the class

Whether you are eligible

Your quest history is stored as a compound: the low 24 bits are the quest index, and two flag bits record success and failure. That is why "not succeeded before" and "not failed recently" are separate, cheap tests — and why a quest you have already done will simply stop being offered. kod/include/blakston.khd:2847–2849

How to actually get quests

  • Be eligible for more of them. The restriction masks key off karma, faction, murderer status and guild rank. A lawful, factioned, guilded character with a broad spell list qualifies for far more of the seventy-five than a neutral loner does.
  • Talk to the ten permanent givers. Their quests are never scheduled and will never come to you.
  • Failing has a memory. Several templates require "has not failed recently." Abandoning a quest closes doors for a while.
  • Several are one-shot forever — Q_PLAYER_NOTSUCCEEDED without the "recently". The scimitar proficiency quest is one of these.
  • Stand close. Five units. A delivery that will not complete is usually a distance problem.
