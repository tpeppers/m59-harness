Qor — the School of Corruption

Qor is the mirror of Shal'ille and costs the same thing in the opposite direction: karma at or below minus ten times the spell's level, which means living at −60 for the deepest magic. It is a night school in the most literal sense — its power is computed from the distance between the current hour and noon — and it is the most expensive polarity in the game to hold, because lawful merchants notice.

Where its power comes from

Spell power is half your ability in the spell plus the bonuses below, on a scale of 1 to 99. It decides your success chance, your mana cost, your casting speed and the strength of the effect — see Spells and Spellcasting.

What the school is for

Killing things. Qor holds the deepest directly offensive magic in the game, and spell damage largely bypasses armour: the flat reduction every worn piece grants is set to zero against a pure spell attack. A fighter's armour is his largest mitigation and it mostly does not apply to you.

kod/object/item/passitem/defmod.kod:110–122

Debilitation. The curse and poison lines take an enemy apart over time rather than at once, which suits a caster who expects to be outlasted in a straight exchange.

Being at −60 karma has consequences outside the spell list. Creatures flagged MOB_KARMA_AG will leave you alone — a real defensive benefit — but lawful merchants become difficult, and you are a legitimate target for anyone who wants to be a hero.

Playing it

  • Play at night. The primary bonus swings from 2 at midday to 26 at midnight on the server's clock. That is a quarter of the entire spell-power scale, for free, for choosing when to fight.
  • Karma feeds the secondary bonus as well as the gate, so a committed Qor caster is stronger than a hesitant one. Half-measures are the worst position on this axis.
  • Moving karma costs at most 1.5 points per monster kill, and only creatures with a positive karma value move you downward. Plan for dozens of kills, not a session.
  • Darkness is worth casting first: it raises the primary bonus for everything you cast inside it.
