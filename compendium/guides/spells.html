Spells and Spellcasting

None of what matters about a spell is sent to your client. The wire tells you a name, how many targets it wants, and which school it belongs to; the mana cost, the reagents, the karma requirement and the entire success calculation live on the server and are never transmitted. That is why this page exists. Everything below is compiled out of the code that enforces it.

The one number that governs everything: spell power

Every question about a cast — will it work, how long will it take, how much will it cost, how strong will the effect be — resolves against a single derived value called spell power, on a scale of 1 to 99. It is not your ability in the spell. Your ability is half of it; the rest is the world.

base = your ability in this spell / 2 primary bonus = a per-school value from the world state, bound 0…30 secondary bonus = a per-school value from you, bound 0…10 faction bonus = from Parliament spell power = base + primary + secondary + faction (in an anti-magic room, minus the aura, plus mana convergence) bound to at least 0 + shrine bonus for this school + the spell modifier of every item you are wearing + any administrative bonus for this school − dementia, if you are under it × (100 + personal modifier) / 100 bound to 1…99

kod/object/passive/spell.kod:2066 GetSpellPower; SPELLPOWER_MINIMUM/MAXIMUM at kod/include/blakston.khd:2089

Because ability is halved and the bonuses are not, a mid-ability caster standing in the right place at the right time can out-cast a high-ability caster standing in the wrong one. The world-state bonus is up to 30 points on a 99-point scale.

What each school draws its power from

This is the real character of the six schools, and it is nowhere in the game's own text.

SchoolPrimary bonus (0–30) — the worldSecondary bonus (0–10) — you
Shal'illeThe room's natural setting; boosted further inside a Forces of Light enchantmentThe magnitude of your karma / 10
QorDistance from noon: 2 + abs(hour − 12) × 2. Strongest at midnight, weakest at midday. Boosted by a Darkness enchantmentThe magnitude of your karma / 10
KraananThe number of active beings in the room — a crowded battleYour current health as a fraction of maximum, × 10
FarenThe room's water and fireYour vigour / 20
RiijaThe height of the moon in the sky / 12How full your hands are: bulk_held × 10 / bulk_max
JalaThe level of the instrument you are holding (this one can reach 40)Your maximum health / 12

kod/object/passive/spell.kod:2107–2196

Read down that table and the schools stop being colours and become playstyles. Qor casters work at night. Kraanan casters are strongest healthy and surrounded. Riija casters gain power by carrying more — the only school in the game that rewards being loaded down. Jala casters must hold an instrument, which is why they cannot also hold a weapon.

Kraanan gets one more concession: negative item modifiers are halved for it, because it is a combat school and expects you to be wearing armour.

Whether the cast succeeds

requisite = your attribute for this school (table below) num = ((100 − requisite) × spell_power / 100) + requisite modified by any Jala hinder spell in the room bound to 5…95 if the spell has exactly one Battler target and you have no line of sight: d = squared_distance / 4 num = num / 2 if d > num/2 num = num − d otherwise success if random(1,100) ≤ num

kod/object/passive/spell.kod:1174 SuccessChance

The requisite attribute is a floor: at spell power 0 your chance still equals your attribute. It never drops below 5% and never rises above 95%.

SchoolRequisite attributeLearning division
Shal'ille, Qor, FarenMysticismMagic
KraananStaminaCombat
Riija, JalaIntellectLore

kod/object/passive/spell.kod:376 GetDivision, :392 GetDivisionReq

This is a character-creation decision, not a play decision. A Kraanan caster wants stamina — which is also the health attribute — so Kraanan is the only school that does not compete with survivability. A Riija or Jala caster wants intellect, which also speeds learning and damps atrophy. A Shal'ille, Qor or Faren caster wants mysticism, which buys nothing else. See Attributes.

A reagent ring in use rescues a failed cast: if the roll fails and the ring is being worn, the spell succeeds anyway.

What a cast costs

mana = viMana − 1 if spell power > 40 − 1 if spell power > 80 − a percentage if you carry a Princess soldier's shield bound to at least 1 (a spell declared at 0 mana stays free)

kod/object/passive/spell.kod:1544 GetManaCost

Reagents are consumed on success. A failed cast costs half the mana, half the exertion, and no reagents — so failure is cheap in materials and expensive in time.

kod/object/passive/spell.kod:1256 PayCosts

The casting trance

trance_time = viCast_time × (150 − spell_power) / 100

kod/object/passive/spell.kod:1883 GetTranceTime

At spell power 1 a cast takes 149% of its declared time; at 99 it takes 51%. A powerful caster is very nearly twice as fast as a weak one, which compounds with everything else on this page.

The karma gate

Qor requires karma ≤ level × −10 Shal'ille requires karma ≥ level × +10 the other four schools have no requirement

kod/object/passive/spell.kod:482 GetRequiredKarma, enforced at :456 KarmaCheck

Checked at cast time, not at learning time. You can own a spell you cannot use. See Karma for how the number moves and how expensive each polarity is to hold.

Learning a spell

Spells are bought from teachers — see who teaches what. You do not start at zero:

initial ability = bound( random(requisite/2, requisite) / 2, 1, 25 )

kod/object/passive/spell.kod:446 InitialAbility

So a caster with mysticism 50 begins a new Shal'ille spell somewhere between ability 6 and 12, and one with mysticism 10 begins at 2 or 3. The attribute pays out at the very first cast and keeps paying.

From there ability rises with use, through two gates, capped at ten improvements per 15–22 minutes across everything you own — and it falls again for anything you neglect. The full mechanism is on Advancing Your Character.

Where you cannot cast

  • Anti-magic rooms subtract an aura value from your spell power directly.
  • Safe zones refuse any spell whose vbCastable_in_HappyLand is false — the harmful ones.
  • Arenas refuse spells that declare SpellBannedInArena.
  • Line of sight is required for full effect on a single-target spell; without it your chance is roughly halved, scaled by distance.

kod/object/passive/spell.kod:505 CanCastHere, :2204, :2255

Rate limits, and the silence around them

You may cast once per second, and the server drops anything past five requests per second without saying so. A cast that exceeds either limit does not fail — it never happened. There is no message.

kod/object/active/holder/nomoveon/battler/player.kod:5305 IsOkayAttackTime · kod/object/active/holder/nomoveon/battler/player/user.kod:50, :883

Spell damage against armour

Spells carry an ATCK_SPELL_ type — fire, cold, shock, holy, unholy, acid, quake. Armour's flat damage reduction is zeroed entirely against pure spell damage and cut to two thirds when an attack carries both a weapon and a spell type. Only typed resistances reach magic, and most armour has none.

kod/object/item/passitem/defmod.kod:110–122

That is the strategic case for a caster: the target's armour, which is the single largest mitigation in the game against a fighter, mostly does not apply to you.
