Attributes

Meridian 59 gives every character six attributes — Might, Intellect, Stamina, Agility, Mysticism, Aim — set once at character creation from a 200-point budget and never changeable afterward by any means available to a mortal player. They are not a soft flavour layer: Aim and Agility are worth 4 points each of raw offense and defense, Stamina sets the hard ceiling on your hit points, Mysticism sets your mana pool and its refill rate, Might is your carrying capacity and your melee damage multiplier, and Intellect alone decides how many schools of magic you will ever be allowed to master.

How the value is computed

Each attribute is stored as two numbers: a permanent base (piMight) and a running modifier (piMightMod) that items, spells and diseases push around. The value the game actually uses is the sum, clamped to 1..70.

GetMight() = bound(piMight + piMightMod, 1, MAXIMUM_STAT) GetIntellect() = bound(piIntellect + piIntellectMod, 1, MAXIMUM_STAT) GetAgility() = bound(piAgility + piAgilityMod, 1, MAXIMUM_STAT) GetAim() = bound(piAim + piAimMod, 1, MAXIMUM_STAT) GetStamina() = bound(piStamina + piStaminaMod, 1, MAXIMUM_STAT) GetMysticism() = bound(piMysticism + piMysticismMod, 1, MAXIMUM_STAT) MAXIMUM_STAT = 70

kod/object/active/holder/nomoveon/battler/player.kod:116, 6369–6397

There is a second family of accessors, GetRawMight and friends, that returns the base value only and clamps at 50 instead of 70:

GetRawIntellect() = bound(piIntellect, 1, 50)

kod/object/active/holder/nomoveon/battler/player.kod:6399–6427

Only one thing in the whole game reads the raw value: the Intellect learn-point budget (player.kod:10839, 11779, 11857). Every point of Intellect from a diamond pendant, a Riija charm or a prince's shield therefore helps you improve skills and resist atrophy, but buys you zero extra school capacity. If you plan to be a generalist mage, the Intellect has to be bought at character creation.

Character creation: the exact trade-off

The character-creation dialog gives you six sliders. Every slider starts at 25, has a floor of 1 and a ceiling of 50, and every point costs exactly one point out of a spare pool of 50.

So the trade is strictly one-for-one and zero-sum: every point you put above 25 in one attribute must come out of another attribute, point for point. Dropping a stat from 25 to 1 frees 24 points; the theoretical extreme is five stats at 1 and one at 50 (total 55), which simply wastes 145 points — there is no refund and no benefit to under-spending.

Sent to the server as a 6-element list, in this order: 1 Might, 2 Intellect, 3 Stamina, 4 Agility, 5 Mysticism, 6 Aim

kod/object/active/holder/nomoveon/battler/player.kod:2063, 2091–2096; module/char/charstat.c:24–29

The server re-validates. If the list is not exactly six long, or any entry is below 1 or above 50, or the total exceeds 200, it does not clamp — it hands you a deliberately ruined character:

piMight = 3; piIntellect = 1; piStamina = 4; piAgility = 1; piMysticism = 5; piAim = 9; % total 23

kod/object/active/holder/nomoveon/battler/player.kod:2064–2088

The creation dialog says it plainly: "Set your character's statistics. Choose wisely; these statistics are permanent." (module/char/char.rc:528). Nothing in the kod ever writes piMight, piIntellect, piStamina, piAgility, piMysticism or piAim for a player after character creation except DM, which sets all six to 50 for immortals (kod/object/active/holder/nomoveon/battler/player/user/dm.kod:1130–1135). Modifiers are wiped, not preserved, whenever the character is reset or recalibrated: ZeroMods sets all six ...Mod properties back to 0 (player.kod:11689–11698), and is called from ResetCharacter (player.kod:2320) and from the recalibration sweep (player.kod:10437). Your stat bars in the client are drawn with a range of 0..50 even though the in-play cap is 70 (kod/object/active/holder/nomoveon/battler/player/user.kod:6686, 6706, 6729, 6749, 6769, 6789 send min 0 / max 50; the bar range is set from those in module/merintr/statmain.c:128). A soldier at rank 7 wearing the matching charm can push a stat to 57 and see a bar that looks identical to 50. The extra points are real and are used by every formula on this page — the display just cannot show them.

Melee damage

Every melee stroke runs its damage through DamageFactors:

damage = (proficiency + 1) * viMaxProficiencyDamage / 100 % weapon proficiency part + ((100 + bound(Might - 25, 0, 40)) * damage) / 100 % Might part viMaxProficiencyDamage = 5 by default

kod/object/passive/skill/stroke.kod:67, 332–353

So the Might multiplier is 1.00 at Might 25 or below, +1% per point from 26 up, and hard capped at +40% (Might 65). Since creation caps you at 50, the best a player can buy at creation is +25% melee damage; the remaining 15% is only reachable with charms, shields or Superstrength.

MightMelee damage multiplier
1–25100%

40115%
50125%
65–70140% (cap)

Might does not improve your chance to hit — GetOffense never reads it (player.kod:4227–4291). Might is pure damage-per-connected-hit plus inventory space. It also does nothing at all for ranged attacks (Fire overrides DamageFactors to use Aim, fire.kod:165–182) and nothing for touch-attack spells (TouchAttackSpell.DamageFactors returns the damage unchanged, touchatk.kod:144–149).

Aim

Offense

iOffense = (stroke_ability * 3) + (proficiency * 2) + (Aim * 4) + (base_max_health * 3 / 2) ... then weapon, enchantment and faction modifiers ... iOffense = bound(iOffense, 1, 1000)

kod/object/active/holder/nomoveon/battler/player.kod:4241–4290

Offense turns into a hit chance against the target's defense:

chance_to_hit = bound( (attacker_offense * 55) / defender_defense, 10, 95 ) % percent EQUAL_CHANCE_HIT = 55

kod/object/active/holder/nomoveon/battler.kod:19, 330–335

Worked example. A player with slash ability 60, sword proficiency 60 and 100 base max health, attacking something with 550 defense:

AimOffenseChance to hit a 550-defense target
10180 + 120 + 40 + 150 = 490490×55/550 = 49%
25180 + 120 + 100 + 150 = 55055%
50180 + 120 + 200 + 150 = 65065%

Forty points of Aim is worth 160 offense, or about 16 percentage points of hit chance in this bracket. For reference, 160 offense is the same as 53 points of stroke ability or 80 points of weapon proficiency — more than either skill can ever supply, since both cap at 99.

The requisite stat is used in four separate places, so it matters far more than a single formula would suggest:

1. Starting ability when you first learn the skill InitialAbility = bound( random(req/2, req) / 2, 1, 25 ) [skill.kod:184-186] 2. Raw success chance of using the skill at ability A num = ((100 - req) * A / 100) + req % percent [skill.kod:228-233] 3. Base chance to improve on a use increase_chance = 60 + req - (skill_level * 10) - generalization_penalty [skill.kod:399-406] 4. Floor on the improve roll, and the SOFT CAP increase_chance = bound(increase_chance, 1 + req/10, 99) [skill.kod:363-364] if ability > (2*req - 1): final = final / SOFTCAP_PENALTY (5 for skills) [skill.kod:18, 426-429]

The soft cap is the reason low requisite stats hurt so much. Once a skill's ability exceeds 2 × requisite_stat - 1, every further improvement roll is divided by 5 (skills) or 4 (spells). At the requisite stat 50 the threshold is 99, i.e. it never bites. At 25 the threshold is 49 — the whole second half of that skill's progression runs at one fifth speed. At 10 it is 19.

Stamina

Stamina is the survivability stat and, indirectly, the single biggest contributor to combat power, because base max health feeds both offense and defense at 1.5 points each.

The hit-point ceiling

kod/object/active/holder/nomoveon/battler/player.kod:5897–5906

Everyone starts at 20 (player.kod:2101–2105).

Because the hard cap is 150 and 100 + 50 = 150, a Stamina-50 character reaches the absolute ceiling with base stats alone. Stamina above 50 raises no ceiling — it only helps the other Stamina effects below.

How fast you gain those hit points

After a qualifying kill the server rolls random(1, highmark) against your accumulated gain chance:

index = (base_max_health * (100 - Stamina)) / 100 highmark = (index + 1) * index roll: random(1, highmark) < gain_chance + bound((monster_level - base_max_health)/5, 0, 10) and requires base_max_health < 101 + Stamina

Death penalties

When you die, every skill and every spell you know above ability 5 is rolled for a 1- or 2-point loss (2 points if you are flagged a murderer):

if ability > 5 and random(1,100) > Stamina and random(1,100) < piDeathCost % piDeathCost is normally 100 then lose points

kod/object/active/holder/nomoveon/battler/player.kod:8279–8308

The Stamina clause means each eligible entry escapes with probability Stamina/100. At Stamina 50, half your skills and spells survive each death untouched; at Stamina 10, 90% of them take the hit.

Intellect

How many schools you can master

This is the big one, and it is the only stat effect that is genuinely irreversible in its consequences. Every school of magic, plus the weapon-skill division, contributes learn points equal to the highest level you know in it:

level: 1 2 3 4 5 6 learn points: 1 2 4 6 8 10

kod/util/system.kod:414 (vlLevelPoints), 6224

Learning a new level is gated by:

iPoints = sum over the 7 divisions of GetLevelLearnPoints(highest level known) (+ the marginal cost of the new level, if you know nothing in that school yet) iNeed = (iPoints * POINTS_SLOPE) + (297 - GetMaxLearnPoints() * POINTS_SLOPE) - ((GetRawIntellect() * 2 * POINTS_SLOPE) / 5) iNeed = bound(iNeed, MIN_NEEDED_TO_ADVANCE, $) if previous level had <3 spells: iNeed = iNeed/3 (level 1) or iNeed*2/3 (level 2) you may learn iff iHave >= iNeed where iHave = sum of your top three abilities in the previous level (max 297), or a flat 297 when learning a level-1 spell POINTS_SLOPE = 7 ; MIN_NEEDED_TO_ADVANCE = 75 ; piMaxLearnPoints = 16

player.kod:93, 97, 10781, 10786, 10813–10846, 10925; kod/util/settings.kod:71, 261–264

Substituting the shipped settings (piMaxLearnPoints = 16, POINTS_SLOPE = 7) gives iNeed = 7×iPoints + 185 − (Intellect×14)/5. Setting iHave to its maximum of 297 and solving gives the clean result the code uses directly elsewhere:

maximum total learn points you can ever hold = 16 + (RawIntellect * 2) / 5

kod/object/active/holder/nomoveon/battler/player.kod:11778–11779, 11856–11857

Speed of improvement

GetInitialChance(who) = viChance_to_increase * (100 + Intellect) / 100 (+ faction bonus)

kod/object/passive/skill.kod:377–393; kod/object/passive/spell.kod:1693–1709

This is the first gate on every improvement roll for every skill and every spell: fail it and no improvement is even attempted. Intellect 50 gives a flat +50% to the frequency of improvement attempts over Intellect 0, and +49% over Intellect 1.

Notice what appears in both the offense and defense formulas: bmh*3/2, your base max health. 100 base health is worth 150 offense and 150 defense — equivalent to 37 points of Aim and 37 points of Agility simultaneously. Recommendations by playstyle

Every recommendation below is a consequence of the cited formulas, not folklore. The one constant: Stamina is never a dump stat. It sets a permanent ceiling on the quantity that feeds both offense and defense, and low Stamina multiplies the grind to reach even that lower ceiling by up to 3.2× (player.kod:5903, 7900, 4258, 4320). All three of the game's own presets put Stamina at 50 for a reason (charstat.c:34–38).

Melee warrior

Stamina 50, Aim 50, Might 40-50, Agility 25-30, Intellect 10-20, Mysticism 1-10

Aim before Might: 4 offense per point is a bigger swing than 1% damage per point, because a miss does zero damage. Might still matters — it is your only damage multiplier and it gates axe, hammer and mace proficiency — but only above 25 (stroke.kod:350). If you intend to use swords, short swords or bows, Aim is doing double duty as both offense and the proficiency requisite (profswrd.kod:40, profshsw.kod:43, archery.kod:44), which raises your proficiency soft cap to 99. Keep enough Intellect to hold Fencing at 6 plus one useful school: 10 Intellect gives a 20-point budget, exactly Fencing 6 + one school at 6.

Archer

Aim 50, Stamina 50, Agility 30, Might 25-35, Intellect 20, Mysticism 10-15

Aim 50 is non-negotiable: it is offense (+4/pt), ranged damage (+1%/pt above 25), the requisite for both Archery and the Fire stroke, and therefore your bow soft cap (fire.kod:138, 178; archery.kod:44; player.kod:4257). Might buys nothing for a bow — Fire.DamageFactors ignores it entirely (fire.kod:165–182) — so spend on Might only for carrying capacity and a melee backup weapon.

Pure mage

Mysticism 50, Intellect 40-50, Stamina 50, Might 20-25, Agility 20-25, Aim 1-15

Mysticism is mana, mana rate, spell success and spell improvement all at once (player.kod:5967, 5656; spell.kod:398), and at 50 it also gives you an effective touch-attack proficiency of 75 without ever training Punch (touchatk.kod:172). Intellect is what decides whether you end up with two schools or four (player.kod:11779). If you want breadth — Shal'ille and Qor and Riija and Jala — Intellect 50 for a 36-point budget is the only way to get there, and it can never be bought later, because the budget reads GetRawIntellect. If you want depth in two schools, 16–25 Intellect is enough and the spare points are better spent elsewhere. Note that Kraanan is a Stamina school and Riija/Jala are Intellect schools (spell.kod:392–417), so "Mysticism is the mage stat" is only true for Qor, Shal'ille and Faren.

Nuke mage who never melees

Mysticism 50, Stamina 50, Intellect 35-45, Agility 25-35, Might 10-20, Aim 10-20

Aim can be dumped — touch-attack spells substitute Mysticism for proficiency and their hit roll comes from the spell ability, not Aim (touchatk.kod:166–188). Do not dump Agility below about 25: it is the requisite stat for Dodge, Parry and Block (skill.kod:288), and at Agility 25 those skills stop improving properly at ability 49 because of the soft cap (skill.kod:426–429). Keep Might high enough to carry reagents — 20 Might is 2100 units of bulk (player.kod:10458).

Hybrid / fighter-mage

Stamina 50, Aim 40, Might 40, Mysticism 35, Agility 25, Intellect 10

This is close to the shipped Hybrid preset. The mathematics of the learn budget punish hybrids hardest: Fencing at 6 costs 10 points out of a 20-point budget at Intellect 10, which leaves exactly one school mastered and nothing else. If you want Fencing 6 plus two schools at 6, you need 16 + (Int*2)/5 ≥ 30, i.e. Intellect 35 or higher. Decide that at the character screen or not at all.

Faction soldier

If you plan to carry a soldier's shield, remember it will hand you +5 in two specific stats at rank 7 (reblshld.kod:120–148: Might and Aim; dukeshld.kod: Stamina and Agility; prinshld.kod: Intellect and Mysticism). Those points count for everything except the Intellect learn budget, which reads only the base value. Planning to reach 50 in a stat "with the shield" works for Aim, Agility, Might, Stamina and Mysticism; it does not work for Intellect.

The one number that answers "did I ruin my character?"

No stat can be changed, so the honest test is whether the permanent ceilings you bought are the ones your playstyle needs. There are exactly four permanent, unrecoverable ceilings set at character creation:

  1. Hit points: min(100 + Stamina, 150) (player.kod:5903–5905). Stamina 20 means 120 HP forever, which is 45 less offense and 45 less defense than a Stamina-50 character, permanently.
  2. Schools: 16 + (RawIntellect*2)/5 learn points (player.kod:11779). No item, spell or quest raises this.
  3. Mana: 15 + Mysticism/5 plus node melds (player.kod:5967) — charms and shields do raise this one, via ComputeMaxMana (player.kod:6122).
  4. Soft caps: each skill or spell stops improving efficiently at 2 × requisite_stat - 1 ability (skill.kod:426–429, spell.kod:1749–1753). Requisite-stat modifiers from items do count here, so a base 45 plus a +5 charm reaches the 99 threshold.

Everything else — damage, offense, defense, carry weight, regeneration — scales smoothly and can be partially bought back with charms, shields and enchantments.
