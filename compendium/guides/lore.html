The World of Meridian

Everything on this page is the game's own text — the strings the server sends when you look at something, the descriptions written into the class files, the words the lieges say when you hand them a token. Nothing here is invented, and where the game is silent it says so. The world's shape is a murdered Duke, three claims to a throne, six gods with incompatible demands, and an Underworld that does not let go easily.

Three claims to a crown

The old Duke was murdered. What follows is the political layer of the game, and the game introduces its three claimants in their own words.

Duke Akardius

Despite being only 16 years old Duke Akardius skillfully maneuvered himself into position to succeed his murdered uncle. The young Duke simply radiates confidence and power. Rumor has it that he is a zealous devotee of Riija and his cunning smile suggests the manipulative, deceitful aspect of the Trickster God. In spite of his lavish and very public displays of mourning for his Uncle, there are some who doubt the sincerity of his grief.

kod/object/active/holder/nomoveon/battler/monster/factions/duke.kod:22 duke_desc_rsc

There is a second description, used when you find him standing over his uncle's body: his face "awash in grief," and "a core of iron will that suggests strength and cunning greater than you have ever seen in a boy of only sixteen years." The game shows you the murder scene and then tells you the boy is a devotee of the god of deception. It does not say he did it.

ibid., duke_murder_desc_rsc

When you bring him a councillor's token, he says: "I will see that this gets returned to its rightful councilor. Of course, he will owe me." The whole political system is debts, and he is the one who says it out loud.

Princess Kateriina

Age has made Princess Kateriina stronger and more certain of herself, but has done little to temper her naivete. She has developed great disdain for the Duke and all who engage in political maneuvering. She considers matters of political intrigue to be below her, trusting that if she rules wisely and justly all will accept her. Magic continues to fascinate her and she sometimes dreams of running away to study the arcane arts.

kod/object/active/holder/nomoveon/battler/monster/factions/princess.kod princess_desc_rsc

She speaks in archaic second person — "Since thou possess a like mind, please support my faction. Put thy name to the roll hither." — where the Duke speaks like a modern politician. Her fascination with magic is not only characterisation: the Princess soldier's shield is the item that reduces spellcasting mana costs, and her faction is the one with the price discount.

kod/object/passive/spell.kod:1578 · kod/util/parlia.kod:1144

Jonas D'Accor, the Rebel

Time and anger have weathered Jonas D'Accor into a hard man. He stands steadfastly in place, contemplating the future of the lands. The actions of the other lieges have forced him to make an attempt for the crown. He believes that the other lieges are not strong enough to rule, even if they have a rightful claim to the throne. He organizes his rebels and hopes to gain support in his quest to take the crown and bring peace and order back to the lands.

kod/object/active/holder/nomoveon/battler/monster/factions/rebel.kod RebelLiege_desc_rsc

The rebel leader is the one who refuses murderers: "I am a servant of the Law, and as such my conscience will not allow me to help you." The rebellion against the crown is the faction with a conscience about killing, which is the sort of joke the game makes quietly and does not explain.

There are three quest chains named QST_ID_MURDER_PRINCESS_1…3, QST_ID_MURDER_DUKE_1…3 and QST_ID_MURDER_JONAS_1…3. The game is prepared for any of the three to be assassinated, by players, in the same structure that killed the old Duke. kod/include/blakston.khd:2760–2768

The six gods

The clearest statement of what each god is comes from their priestesses, who are written as people rather than as doctrine.

Shal'ille — Priestess Xiana

Clean and prim, this priestess of peaceful serenity speaks only kind words. She even wishes peace on those of Qor and prays for their redemption.

kod/object/active/holder/nomoveon/battler/monster/temples/shalprst.kod

Qor — Priestess Zuxana

The perfect servant of Qor the Vile. Shadows deepen where this malevolent woman stands. She has a devious sensuality that she will exploit to turn men and women to succumb to the dark will of Qor. If she can not seduce others to Qor, she would rather see them dead.

kod/object/active/holder/nomoveon/battler/monster/temples/qorprst.kod

Note the asymmetry the game builds in: Shal'ille's priestess prays for Qor's followers to be redeemed; Qor's priestess would rather Shal'ille's were dead. That asymmetry is enforced mechanically — the two schools' karma gates make them mutually exclusive, and it is the Qor caster who must go hunting good creatures to stay eligible.

kod/object/passive/spell.kod:482 · see Karma

Kraanan — Priestess Qerti'nya

Qerti'nya is very judgmental of the strength of those who come before her. Blood and hellfire are the tools of this priestess, for only the strong and good meaning deserve to live. She is quick to make accusations or statements. Although she delivers a powerful sermon, she is likely to go out drinking with the men after it is over.

kod/object/active/holder/nomoveon/battler/monster/temples/kranprst.kod

The war god's priestess goes drinking afterwards. Kraanan is the school whose spell power rises with the number of people in the room and with how healthy you are — a god of company and of vigour, not of grimness.

Faren — Priestess Tenuv'vyal

Hair unkempt and anger barely checked. Some say she is on the brink of madness, others say she is a terrible menace waiting to be released like a raging storm. She despises civilization and feels humans must return to the ways of the wilds.

kod/object/active/holder/nomoveon/battler/monster/temples/farnprst.kod

Riija — the Monk

Floating several feet above the ground, this enigma of a being watches you. When it speaks, the monk's voice crackles like century old parchment. You have a very uneasy feeling that all is not as it seems.

kod/object/active/holder/nomoveon/battler/monster/temples/kcrjmonk.kod

Riija's servant is the only one the game will not call a person. "This enigma of a being." "All is not as it seems" — which is both characterisation and a warning, since Riija is the school of illusion and the Duke is said to be its devotee. See Riija.

Jala

Jala has no temple priestess in the same pattern. It is the bard's god, reached through the Minstrel and through instruments, and its presence in the world is a song: when two bards compete, the game says "A nearby bard of Jala begins to sing a song, but quits when they realize they can't be heard over the song sung by another."

The Koc'atan

Something the compendium surfaces that is easy to miss in play: many creatures carry a second name, vrKocName, which is what the Koc'atan call them. The ant is yokinich. There is a whole parallel naming of the world's fauna belonging to the island culture, and every bestiary page shows it where it exists.

kod/object/active/holder/nomoveon/battler/monster.kod:170 vrKocName · e.g. monster/ant.kod:19

The Underworld

Death is not an ending here; it is a place. RID_UNDERWORLD is region 1, and it has no ordinary exits. Everything you were carrying stays on your corpse in the world you left. See Death, the Underworld and PvP for the mechanics.

kod/include/blakston.khd:375

Where the game is silent

Three things a reader might expect to find here and will not, because the source does not contain them:

  • Who killed the old Duke. The game shows you the body, tells you the nephew worships the god of deception, and stops.
  • A creation myth or a chronology. There is no dated history in the class files. The gods are described through their servants and their spells, never through a cosmology.
  • What Riija wants. Every other school states a value — compassion, corruption, war, the wild, song. Riija's is left as "all is not as it seems."
The client's string resources under run/localclient/resource/*.rsc and the printed material in manual/ contain further material that this page does not cover; everything above is from the server source, where it can be cited to a line.
