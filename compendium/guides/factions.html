Factions

Three factions compete for control of the realm: the Duke, the Princess and the Rebels. Joining one is not a cosmetic choice — it changes your chance to hit, your defence, your damage, your spell power, your regeneration and your vigour costs, and the size of every one of those bonuses depends on how well your side is currently doing. A faction that is winning makes its members measurably stronger. A faction that is losing makes them measurably weaker.

Who is eligible

The system distinguishes players who are intriguing — genuinely participating in the political layer — from everyone else. To be intriguing you must have player-killing enabled and be either sufficiently advanced in health or sufficiently adept.

kod/object/active/holder/nomoveon/battler/player.kod:11316 PlayerIsIntriguing

A +200 defence bonus is large. Against an offence of 605, raising defence from 585 to 785 drops the attacker's chance to hit from 56% to 42%. Faction standing is one of the biggest single levers on survivability in the game.

How the size of the bonus is decided

Every bonus runs through one function, which scales a base number by two things: how much power your faction holds relative to balance, and how many members it has relative to balance.

balance = total_power_points × balance_factor / 100 power contribution: if power ≤ balance: p = (110 × power / balance) − 10 % line from (0,−10) to (b,100) else: p = 100 + 50 × (power − balance) / balance p = bound(p, −10, 150) membership contribution: mbalance = intriguer_count × balance_factor / 100 if members ≤ mbalance or mbalance < 3: m = 100 else: m = −50 × members / mbalance + 150 m = bound(m, 25, 100) bonus = base × p × m / 10000 if p > 0 = base × p / 100 if p ≤ 0 (membership cannot dull a penalty) unaligned: bonus = bonus / 4, and never below 0

kod/util/parlia.kod:998 CalcFactionBonus

Three consequences, and they are the entire design of the political layer:

  • Power above balance keeps paying, up to 150%. A dominant faction is not merely winning; its members are individually 50% stronger than a balanced one's.
  • Power below balance goes negative. At zero power the contribution is −10%, so members of a collapsing faction are actively weaker than unaligned players, who cannot go negative at all.
  • Crowds dilute. Membership above balance cuts the bonus, down to a floor of 25%. Joining the winning side is self-limiting: the more people who do it, the less each of them gets. But the dilution only applies while power is positive — piling into a losing faction does not soften its penalty at all.

Power stops accruing at twice balance, so there is a ceiling on how dominant a faction can make its members — but no floor on how weak a losing one can make them beyond the −10%.

Faction combat

When two enemy faction soldiers fight, damage between them is increased by 15% — and that increase is applied after both damage caps, so it is the only thing in the game that can take more than 30 health in a single blow.

if the defender is a Player and the attacker's SoldierShield says this is an enemy attack: damage = damage × 115 / 100 % after the one-third cap and MAX_DAMAGE_PER_HIT

kod/object/active/holder/nomoveon/battler/player.kod:4620–4632

The soldier shield is the marker: it is what identifies you as a combatant of your faction and what the damage check consults. Killing an enemy soldier while carrying it also exempts you from the murderer flag that killing an innocent would otherwise set.

kod/object/active/holder/nomoveon/battler/player.kod:4870–4875

Leaving takes a full day. Coming back takes four hours after that. The system is deliberately built so that you cannot follow the power curve from hour to hour — by the time you have switched to the winning side, the standings have been recomputed sixty times.

Other things factions touch

  • Lawful merchants and murderers. Faction pressure can force a merchant flagged MOB_LAWFUL to trade with a murderer who would otherwise be refused. kod/util/parlia.kod:1468 GetFactionSellToMurderer
  • The Princess's shield reduces spell mana costs by a percentage. kod/object/passive/spell.kod:1578
  • Rebels get a flat +1 damage, stated in the source as a small bonus for that faction specifically. kod/util/parlia.kod:1293
  • Faction liege NPCs stand for each side and are recreated by Parliament when the political state changes. kod/util/parlia.kod:379, :588

Should you join?

The honest calculation: a faction at or above balance gives you roughly four times what neutrality does, on six separate statistics. A faction below balance gives you less than neutrality, and neutrality cannot go negative. Joining is a bet on your side's standing over the next twenty-four hours, because that is how long it takes to get back out.

If you are not intriguing — no player-killing, or not yet advanced enough — the question does not arise: you already collect the fixed unaligned bonus and cannot be hurt by any of this.
