Health Regeneration

Health comes back on a self-rescheduling timer, one point at a time, and only if you have done something since you walked into the room. The interval is set by three numbers — Vigor (squared), Stamina (linear), and your maximum health (clamped between 40 and 100) — and it can never go below one second unless a bard is singing Restorate. There is no healing potion, food does nothing for your health, and every external heal in the game goes through one of exactly two doors.

The one thing that surprises people: health regeneration is gated on PFLAG_MOVED_SINCE_ENTRY. Walk into a hostile room and stand perfectly still and you regenerate nothing — the timer fires, sees the flag clear, redraws your health bar and does not give you a point (kod/object/active/holder/nomoveon/battler/player.kod:2640–2662). Mana has no such gate. This is the mechanic behind "why is my health stuck".

The timer

Health ticks on the same kind of self-rescheduling timer as mana. Every tick you gain exactly one point if you are below maximum, or lose exactly one point if you are above it.

HealthTimer(): if piFlags & PFLAG_MOVED_SINCE_ENTRY: if piHealth < piMax_health: GainHealth(1) else: LoseHealth(1, decay=TRUE) else: NewHealth() % redraw only — no point is gained

kod/object/active/holder/nomoveon/battler/player.kod:2640–2662

The timer exists only while piHealth <> piMax_health and you are alive; at exactly full it is deleted, and it is never created at 0 health (player.kod:5837–5852). Every gain and every loss routes through NewHealth, which is what re-arms it.

The movement gate, precisely

PFLAG_MOVED_SINCE_ENTRY (0x400000, kod/include/blakston.khd:2125) is misleadingly named: it means "has acted since entering", not "has moved". It is cleared in only two places and set in only one.

The same flag is what holds monsters off you when you first enter a room, which is why it is set by any action rather than by movement alone — turning on the spot is enough. The practical consequence is that the gate almost never bites in play except in one situation: you log in wounded, or you flee into a corner and freeze. Turn once and the tap opens.

The regeneration formula

CalculateHealthTime() -> milliseconds until the next health point t = ((200 - Vigor) * (200 - Vigor)) / 6 + 1000 t = ((125 - Stamina) * t) / 100 t = (t * 100) / bound(piMax_health, 40, 100) t = t - FactionRegenBonus(you, hpregen=TRUE) if a Restorate song is playing in this room: t = bound(t, 1000, 60000) t = t * (400 - (40 + jalaSpellPower)) / 400 return bound(t, 670, 60000) % note the lower floor return bound(t, 1000, 60000)

kod/object/active/holder/nomoveon/battler/player.kod:5611–5643; Restorate's adjustment at kod/object/passive/spell/jala/restorat.kod:64–72

Compare this with the mana formula. Both divide by Vigor and by a capacity, but the shapes are completely different: mana divides by Vigor linearly and by your entire maximum, while health divides by Vigor squared and by a maximum that stops counting at 100.

Vigor: a quadratic, not a divisor

The leading term is (200 − Vigor)²/6. Because it is a square of the shortfall from the cap, the first points of Vigor you recover are worth very little and the last ones are worth a great deal. Going from 100 to 200 Vigor is not "twice as fast" as it is for mana; it removes the term entirely.

Stamina also sets your maximum-health ceiling, the rate at which you gain health points, and how fast you rest — see Attributes and Advancing. It is the only attribute in this formula; Might, Agility, Aim, Intellect and Mysticism appear nowhere in it.

Maximum health: the divisor that stops at 100

t = (t * 100) / bound(piMax_health, 40, 100)

Below 40 and above 100 the divisor is clamped, and those two clamps do opposite things to you.

Between 40 and 100 maximum health, your time to heal from near-death to full is almost exactly constant. The divisor cancels the extra points exactly. Above 100 it stops cancelling, so every point of maximum health you gain past 100 makes a full heal strictly slower — a 150-hp character takes half again as long to top up as a 100-hp one, at identical Vigor and Stamina. Under 40 it also stops cancelling, in your favour: a 20-hp newbie refills in two minutes.

This is the exact opposite of mana, where the divisor is your whole maximum and melding more nodes therefore multiplies your mana per minute.

Above maximum: the two doors, and the bleed

Every heal in the game enters through one of exactly two messages, and which one it is decides whether it can push you over your maximum.

MessageCeilingReturnsSource
GainHealth2 × piMax_health, with a message when you hit itnothingkod/object/active/holder/nomoveon/battler/player.kod:5777–5793
GainHealthNormalpiMax_health; refuses outright if you are already above itthe amount actually gainedkod/object/active/holder/nomoveon/battler/player.kod:5795–5821

While you are above your maximum, the same timer runs in reverse: one point every CalculateHealthTime milliseconds, flagged decay=TRUE so it does not break a trance.

Over-health bleeds off at your regeneration rate, not at a fixed rate. Mana uses BOOST_DECAY_TIME = 30 s flat for its overcharge (player.kod:63, :5652); health has no equivalent. So the very build that regenerates fastest also loses its vampiric surplus fastest — a Stamina-50 character at Vigor 200 sheds an over-max point every second; a Stamina-25 character at Vigor 25 keeps it for nearly six. If you have just over-healed yourself off a kill, that surplus is worth the most to a low-Vigor character.

One enchantment overrides all of this. While Mark of Dishonor is on you, NewHealth clamps your health to piBase_max_health on every single update, so you cannot hold any surplus at all (kod/object/active/holder/nomoveon/battler/player.kod:5830–5835).

Healing that is not regeneration

This is the complete set of things in the game that add health to a player. Note what is not in it: there is no healing potion — the potion directory contains cures, resistances and utilities but no restorative — and food does nothing for health. EatSomething touches exertion and your stomach and nothing else (kod/object/active/holder/nomoveon/battler/player.kod:5734–5758).

Only five things in the whole tree use the uncapped door, and four of them are vampiric or sacrificial. That is the design: surplus health is something you take from a living thing, not something you drink.

Losing health

LoseHealth breaks a trance unless it is flagged as decay (player.kod:5761–5775) — which is why standing in a poison cloud does not, on its own, break Mana Focus: poison writes to piHealth directly and never calls LoseHealth at all.

Where maximum health comes from

Unlike mana, there is no ComputeMaxHealth: maximum health is not recomputed from sources, it is mutated by whatever touches it, through two different messages.

MessageWhat it changesBoundsLine
GainBaseMaxHealthpiBase_max_health — your permanent progression, this game's "level"bound(x, 20, 100 + Stamina), then hard-capped at 150player.kod:5897–5921
GainMaxHealthpiMax_health — the working value, including temporary effectsbound(x, 20, $); current health is deliberately left aloneplayer.kod:5923–5963

Only four things move the working value away from the base: the circlet, the health boon enchantment, Morph, and a quest-node prize. Anything else is a bug, and the server treats it as one — the periodic recalibration pass logs a complaint and forces piMax_health = piBase_max_health (kod/object/active/holder/nomoveon/battler/player.kod:10406–10411). Logging in with a circlet on triggers a related safety: InitialSetBaseMaxHealth unequips anything that answers AdjustsMaxHealth before it snapshots the base (kod/object/active/holder/nomoveon/battler/player/user.kod:7425–7440).

How the base value grows is the subject of Advancing. The short version: you gain a point by killing things at or above your own maximum health, on a roll against an accumulator, and the ceiling is min(100 + Stamina, 150).

Reading the health bar

The server sends health as a numeric stat with a fixed display range of 0–100 and a separate "current max" limit line at your real maximum — identical in shape to the mana bar.

AddPacket(1,1, 4,user_stat_health, 1,STAT_VALUE, 1,STAT_INTEGER, 4,piHealth, 4,0, 4,100, 4,piMax_health);

kod/object/active/holder/nomoveon/battler/player/user.kod:6643–6648; the client maps these to GRPH_RANGESET / GRPH_POSSET / GRPH_LIMITSET at module/merintr/statnum.c:196–198

So the bar is drawn against a hard 0–100 scale with a marker at your maximum. Below 100 max health the marker sits short of the end and the bar reads as a fraction of 100, not of your maximum; above 100 — and certainly at the 2× over-heal ceiling — the bar pegs and stops telling you anything. The redraw fires from NewHealth, which every gain and loss calls.

Quick reference: how to heal faster

  1. Act once when you enter a room. A single turn sets PFLAG_MOVED_SINCE_ENTRY and starts the tap. Inns and guild halls never clear it, so you always regenerate there.
  2. Eat. Vigor enters squared and resting stops at 80. Going from 80 to 200 Vigor is worth roughly 3× your health regeneration — a bigger single lever than any other on this page. See Vigor.
  3. Stamina at creation. It is the only attribute in the formula, it is worth up to 1.8×, and it also sets your health ceiling and your resting speed.
  4. Know where the floor is. Past roughly Vigor 140–166, a Stamina-50 veteran is pinned at 1000 ms per point and further Vigor does nothing for health at all — though it keeps helping mana, which has no such floor.
  5. Do not chase maximum health past 100 for survivability alone. Every point beyond 100 lengthens a full heal without shortening a tick. It is still worth taking for the offense and defense it buys (see Attributes) — just not for the regeneration.
  6. A bard changes the rules. Restorate is the only thing that breaks the one-second floor, down to 670 ms — 90 health per minute.
