The Six Schools of Magic

Six gods, six schools, 150 castable spells. Two of the schools sit at opposite ends of a karma axis and lock each other out; the other four are open to anybody. But the decision that actually shapes a caster is quieter than polarity: each school draws its power from a different feature of the world, and each requires a different attribute — chosen at character creation and never changeable.

Every school runs levels 1 through 6. A further 24 spell classes belong to the administrative pseudo-school and are not castable by players; one more sits outside the schools entirely.

The polarity axis

Shal'ille and Qor are the same mechanism with opposite signs, and the sign is checked every time you cast:

Shal'ille level 6 needs karma ≥ +60 Qor level 6 needs karma ≤ −60 karma runs −100 … +100

There is no karma at which both are available at any level. A caster who wants either school's deep spells commits their character's alignment to it, and alignment is moved mainly by what they kill — at most 1.5 points per monster. Choosing a polar school is choosing what you will spend the next several hundred kills fighting. See Karma.

The four neutral schools cost nothing in alignment and can be mixed freely with each other and with either polar school, up to the level your karma allows.

What each school draws power from

This is the real difference between them, and the game never states it anywhere a player can read. Spell power — which sets your success chance, your mana cost, your casting speed and the strength of the effect — is half your ability in the spell plus up to 40 points of bonus, and the bonus comes from somewhere different in each school.

Kraanan gets one further concession, stated in the source as being because it is a combat school: negative spell modifiers from worn items are halved for it. Every other school pays the full armour penalty. That, plus stamina as its requisite attribute, makes Kraanan the only school a heavily armoured fighter can carry without compromise.

Mysticism — required by three of the six schools — buys nothing else in the game. Stamina and intellect both do double duty. If you are undecided between two schools of similar appeal, the one whose requisite attribute you were going to buy anyway is worth more than it looks. See Attributes.

Learning

Spells are bought from teachers, and the schools are taught in different places by different people — see who teaches what, sorted by level so the affordable rows come first.
