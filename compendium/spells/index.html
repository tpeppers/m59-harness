How to read this

Karma is not a stored property of a spell. It is derived from school and level by Spell.GetRequiredKarma: Qor demands karma at or below −10 × level, Shal’ille demands karma at or above +10 × level, and the other four schools demand nothing. Karma runs −100 to +100, so a level-6 Qor spell needs you more than half way to the floor — and locks you out of Shal’ille entirely.
