Block is a basic skill, allowing people in combat to block blows with a shield. A successfully blocked blow will be more damaging to the attacking weapon and to the defending shield.

What kind of skill this is

A skill proper is something you invoke, or that fires automatically when its condition is met. It costs vigour, it can fail, and its ability is both the success chance and the size of the effect.

Learning and improving it

Taught by Rebel Liege, Jonas D'Accor.

Ability runs 0–99 and rises by use against something hard enough to matter, with a 60% roll on each qualifying use. It also atrophies: stop using a skill and the number falls. See Advancing Your Character.
