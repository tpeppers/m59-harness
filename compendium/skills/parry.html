Parrying allows people with a weapon to turn aside a blow, thus avoiding being hit.

What kind of skill this is

A skill proper is something you invoke, or that fires automatically when its condition is met. It costs vigour, it can fail, and its ability is both the success chance and the size of the effect.

Learning and improving it

Taught by Rook.

Ability runs 0–99 and rises by use against something hard enough to matter, with a 20% roll on each qualifying use. It also atrophies: stop using a skill and the number falls. See Advancing Your Character.
