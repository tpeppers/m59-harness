Slash is the most common weapon skill. It helps you hit with melee weapons.

What kind of skill this is

A stroke is a way of swinging. You do not invoke it; the weapon picks one, and its ability decides both how often you connect and how hard. Improving a stroke improves every weapon that uses it.

What it is worth in a fight

damage = weapon_base × 80 / 100

Learning and improving it

Taught by Rook.

Ability runs 0–99 and rises by use against something hard enough to matter, with a 20% roll on each qualifying use. It also atrophies: stop using a skill and the number falls. See Advancing Your Character.

Behaviour overridden here

GetAttackType(weapon_used = $)

{
      local i;
      
      i= send(weapon_used,@GetAttackType);
      
      if (i & ATCK_WEAP_THRUST) AND NOT (i & ATCK_WEAP_SLASH)
      {
         i = i & (~ATCK_WEAP_THRUST);
         i = (i | ATCK_WEAP_SLASH);
      }
      
      return i;
   }
