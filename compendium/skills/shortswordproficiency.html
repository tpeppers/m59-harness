Allows added proficiency with short swords.

What kind of skill this is

A proficiency is a weapon class. It contributes to your chance to hit and adds a small flat bonus to damage — (proficiency + 1) × 5 / 100 per swing — and it is what the weapon checks before you can use it well at all.

Weapons that use it

sword of Riija, short sword

Learning and improving it

No teacher in the world data offers this skill. It is either granted at creation, awarded by a quest, or reserved for monsters.

Ability runs 0–99 and rises by use against something hard enough to matter, with a 60% roll on each qualifying use. It also atrophies: stop using a skill and the number falls. See Advancing Your Character.
