This skill offers added expertise in swords with long blades, magical or not.

What kind of skill this is

A proficiency is a weapon class. It contributes to your chance to hit and adds a small flat bonus to damage — (proficiency + 1) × 5 / 100 per swing — and it is what the weapon checks before you can use it well at all.

Weapons that use it

black dagger, gold sword, Sword of the Hunt, long sword, mystic sword, nerudite sword, Dark Blade of Roq

Learning and improving it

Taught by Rook.

Ability runs 0–99 and rises by use against something hard enough to matter, with a 30% roll on each qualifying use. It also atrophies: stop using a skill and the number falls. See Advancing Your Character.
