This is the most basic hand-to-hand combat move. Can be very strong for a powerful warrior, but is extremely tiring.

What kind of skill this is

A stroke is a way of swinging. You do not invoke it; the weapon picks one, and its ability decides both how often you connect and how hard. Improving a stroke improves every weapon that uses it.

What it is worth in a fight

damage = weapon_base × 100 / 100

Learning and improving it

Taught by Rook.

Ability runs 0–99 and rises by use against something hard enough to matter, with a 60% roll on each qualifying use. It also atrophies: stop using a skill and the number falls. See Advancing Your Character.

What it does

  • PlayerWasHitMsg — The stroke may choose its own damage give or receive message. Otherwise player.kod will just choose a default.
  • PlayerKilledSomethingMsg — The stroke may choose its own damage give or receive message. Otherwise player.kod will offer the default 'You killed the whatever' message.
