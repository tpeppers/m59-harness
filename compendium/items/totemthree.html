Minjaka Vase

Antiquarians claim there only to be a few of the priceless Minjaka Vases left in existence. These exquisitely mastered vases use to line the throne room of Castle Victoria during the Minjakan dynasty of explorers in the second century. Being one of a kind, this vase can not be carried in one's possession for very long or tucked away in a chest.
