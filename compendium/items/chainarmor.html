chain armor

Thousands of tiny, steel links make up this chain armor. Although light and reasonably comfortable, it provides a fair measure of protection.

This item's defence bonus is negative: wearing it makes you easier to hit. The trade is that it absorbs damage when a hit lands. Heavy armour in this game buys mitigation with accuracy, not on top of it.
