Gold Urn

Excavated during the construction of the palace of the dark Princess Venya'cyr, the Urn came quickly into her possession as a valued treasure. Ancient rumors suggest the Urn is cursed. The craftsmanship of this priceless relic comes from unknown origins, certainly predating the Meridian settlers. An unusual presence prevents this object from being stored in a chest or held for very long.
