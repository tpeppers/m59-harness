Dark Blade of Roq

The personal blade of Roq the assassin. This blade appears to be identical to the black daggers Roq hands out for the Assassin game. But the dried blood of Duke Kalior along the blade reveals this to be a much more potent weapon. It almost crackles with malevolent energy.
