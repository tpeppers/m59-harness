plate armor

Formed from solid sheets of hardened steel, this armor offers protection from all but the most ferocious assault. It bears the mark of Fehr'loi Qan, a sign of fine workmanship and first rate materials.

This item's defence bonus is negative: wearing it makes you easier to hit. The trade is that it absorbs damage when a hit lands. Heavy armour in this game buys mitigation with accuracy, not on top of it.
