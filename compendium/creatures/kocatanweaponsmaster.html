Kochtal ko'Tulca

An older man who is obviously blind in both eyes. With his every move exacting and his every word said with forethought, this former warrior defines the slow grace of a seasoned hunter. Noted for his keen ear, some say he can hear those things that make no noise, like a frown or a doubting thought.
