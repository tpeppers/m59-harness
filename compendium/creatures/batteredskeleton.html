battered skeleton

A few scraps of rotten flesh cling to this collection of human bones topped by a battered and distinctly nonhuman skull. Sizzling liquid drips from its teeth, leaving small pits in the ground below.You shudder to think what dark ritual brought this abomination into being.

What killing it does to your karma

Its karma is -70, and a kill is treated as an act whose karma is the negative of the victim's — so killing this creature pushes you towards good.

base = your_karma − (−victim_karma) = your_karma − 70 change = −( base³ / 2500 + 5 × base ) / (11 − swing) % hundredths of a karma point bounded to ±150 for a monster kill — at most 1.5 karma per kill
