What killing it does to your karma

Its karma is 20, and a kill is treated as an act whose karma is the negative of the victim's — so killing this creature pushes you towards evil.

base = your_karma − (−victim_karma) = your_karma + 20 change = −( base³ / 2500 + 5 × base ) / (11 − swing) % hundredths of a karma point bounded to ±150 for a monster kill — at most 1.5 karma per kill
