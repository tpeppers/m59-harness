lupogg

This underwater denizen, although hideous and brutish, is rumored to be peaceful in nature, but there is no evidence of that now. The lupoggs seem upset that their territory has been trespassed upon, and seem eager to challenge any they can find.

What killing it does to your karma

Its karma is 75, and a kill is treated as an act whose karma is the negative of the victim's — so killing this creature pushes you towards evil.

base = your_karma − (−victim_karma) = your_karma + 75 change = −( base³ / 2500 + 5 × base ) / (11 − swing) % hundredths of a karma point bounded to ±150 for a monster kill — at most 1.5 karma per kill
