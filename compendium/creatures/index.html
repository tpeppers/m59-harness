What simple mode does not know

Offence, defence and damage already contain your attributes, skills and weapon — but not your armour's flat damage reduction or its typed resistances, because no single number carries those. So Damage to you and Swings to die are shown before armour mitigation, and are pessimistic by however much your armour absorbs. Switch to Detailed to model it.

Both sides swing about once a second, so swings read as seconds. Swings to kill and swings to die already account for missing: they are health ÷ (average damage × hit chance). The damage columns are before any enchantment; armour is applied to what the creature does to you, and nothing is applied to what you do to it, because no creature in the game declares a resistance — only worn items and resist spells do.

verified: no Monster subclass calls AddResistance; the only callers are kod/object/item/passitem/defmod.kod, ring/resring.kod and spell/persench/resist.kod

How the numbers are reached

creature: attack = defence = 3 × level + 60 × difficulty bounded 1…1500 health = Fuzzy(level) or Fuzzy(level × 120/100) at level ≥ 40 damage = Fuzzy(level / random(10,15)) Fuzzy(n) = n − n/4 + random(0, n/2) you: offence = stroke×3 + proficiency×2 + aim×4 + max_health×3/2 + weapon to-hit defence = parry×2 + block×1 + dodge×3 + agility×4 + max_health×3/2 + armour parry counts only with a weapon in hand, block only with a shield damage = ((weapon base + quality) × 80/100) × (100 + bound(might−25,0,40))/100 + (proficiency + 1) × 5/100 either: chance to hit = bound(attacker offence × 55 / defender defence, 10, 95) %

    Level and difficulty are not the same thing

    Level sets how much health a creature has and how hard it hits. Difficulty sets how hard it is to hit and to be hit by — and it is weighted twenty times more heavily than level in that calculation. A level-100 creature at difficulty 0 is a sack of health that swings hard and is easy to hit; a level-40 creature at difficulty 5 is a wall. Sort by You hit to see which is which.

    People and monsters

    The game has no flag that says which is which. What it has is a default: Monster declares viLevel = 25 and viDifficulty = 0, which give attack and defence of exactly 3 × 25 + 60 × 0 = 75. Every shopkeeper, guard, councillor and urchin in the world has been left at that number, and everything meant to fight has been given more. So the split in the Role column is drawn there:

    attack > 75 -> monster attack = 75 -> person (the class default, untouched)

    Merchants and teachers are broken out separately because what they sell matters more than what they are; all of them also sit at exactly 75. One entry falls below the line — the orc pit boss's corpse, at 63 — and is filed with the people, being a body rather than a combatant.

    What should I be fighting?

    Health only grows from killing things above your own level that actually damaged you, so the useful target is the hardest creature you can still beat — not the safest. Set your real build above, press Generate, then sort by Swings to die and work down until the outcome column stops saying you win.
