dusk rat

The air around the rat is dark and thick with evil. The smell of death and disease linger on its fetid breath as it darts towards its target. The origins of this abomination are unknown. The only definite knowledge known is of the deadly poison it carries in its bite.

What killing it does to your karma

Its karma is -40, and a kill is treated as an act whose karma is the negative of the victim's — so killing this creature pushes you towards good.

base = your_karma − (−victim_karma) = your_karma − 40 change = −( base³ / 2500 + 5 × base ) / (11 − swing) % hundredths of a karma point bounded to ±150 for a monster kill — at most 1.5 karma per kill
