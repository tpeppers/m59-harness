Where to find it

No room in the world declares this creature. It is summoned by another creature, placed by an administrator, or left over from content that is no longer reachable.

What killing it does to your karma

Its karma is -25, and a kill is treated as an act whose karma is the negative of the victim's — so killing this creature pushes you towards good.

base = your_karma − (−victim_karma) = your_karma − 25 change = −( base³ / 2500 + 5 × base ) / (11 − swing) % hundredths of a karma point bounded to ±150 for a monster kill — at most 1.5 karma per kill
