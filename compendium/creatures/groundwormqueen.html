groundworm queen

The largest of the groundworms, the queen is a feared monster. After growing over long periods of time, groundworms need increasing amounts of food to sustain themselves. The queens are driven mad by an overwhelming desire to consume food to satiate their immense hunger. Occasionally groundworm queens lay eggs in corpses that hatch into groundworm larva.

What killing it does to your karma

Its karma is -30, and a kill is treated as an act whose karma is the negative of the victim's — so killing this creature pushes you towards good.

base = your_karma − (−victim_karma) = your_karma − 30 change = −( base³ / 2500 + 5 × base ) / (11 − swing) % hundredths of a karma point bounded to ±150 for a monster kill — at most 1.5 karma per kill
