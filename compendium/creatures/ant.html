What killing it does to your karma

Its karma is -10, and a kill is treated as an act whose karma is the negative of the victim's — so killing this creature pushes you towards good.

base = your_karma − (−victim_karma) = your_karma − 10 change = −( base³ / 2500 + 5 × base ) / (11 − swing) % hundredths of a karma point bounded to ±150 for a monster kill — at most 1.5 karma per kill

No karma moves at all in the newbie region, in an arena, or when the victim's karma is neutral. And once you are already further from neutral than your victim in the same direction, killing them does nothing to you.
