The reference character is a mid-game character — 130 max health, dodge 50, parry 40, agility 40, stroke 50, proficiency 50, aim 40, giving defence 585 and offence 605. Those are the same on every page in this bestiary, so the percentages are directly comparable between creatures. Armour is not included — see Armour for what it subtracts.

It attacks with bite damage. Armour resists by type, so what you wear against this creature matters more than how much you wear.

What killing it does to your karma

Its karma is 15, and a kill is treated as an act whose karma is the negative of the victim's — so killing this creature pushes you towards evil.

base = your_karma − (−victim_karma) = your_karma + 15 change = −( base³ / 2500 + 5 × base ) / (11 − swing) % hundredths of a karma point bounded to ±150 for a monster kill — at most 1.5 karma per kill

kod/object/active/holder/nomoveon/battler/player.kod:6491 CalculateKarmaChangeFromAct · :6524 CalculateKarmaChangeFromKill

No karma moves at all in the newbie region, in an arena, or when the victim's karma is neutral. And once you are already further from neutral than your victim in the same direction, killing them does nothing to you.
