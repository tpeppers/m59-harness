Esseldi

A great traveler and explorer, he now hunts for the appearances of the mystical mana nodes. Esseldi attempts to wear the stately dress of the court, yet always manages to tatter them up on his impromptu explorations. He enjoys talking about all of his findings, whether relevant to anyone else or not.

Where to find it

No room in the world declares this creature. It is summoned by another creature, placed by an administrator, or left over from content that is no longer reachable.
