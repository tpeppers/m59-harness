Reagents

27 things are spell components, and nothing in the game says so. An item is a reagent because some spell names it in ResetReagents — there is no reagent flag, no reagent class, no reagent shop sign. Both tables below are derived by inverting all 148 spells that need one.

How components are spent

on a successful cast: every listed reagent is consumed, in the listed quantity on a failed cast: half the mana, half the exertion, NO reagents

kod/object/passive/spell.kod:626 CanPayReagents, :1256 PayCosts

Failing is therefore cheap in materials and expensive only in time. Running out is the real cost: CanPayCosts refuses the cast outright, before the trance, so a caster who has run dry mid-fight simply stops working.

Carrying them

Reagents are ordinary items and cost you ordinary weight and bulk. A caster who wants to stay out for a long session is carrying dozens of them, and bulk is usually the limit that bites first. One school turns that around: Riija's secondary spell-power bonus is bulk_held × 10 / bulk_max — the fuller your pack, the stronger you cast.
