The World Map

Outdoor rooms in Meridian 59 tile: walk off the north edge of one and you arrive at the south edge of another. That relation is declared in the source, so the map below is not a drawing — it is those declarations, laid out by walking the compass directions from the best-connected room outward. Every box is a link.

Of 266 rooms, 115 have at least one compass exit and can be placed. The rest connect only by an interior exit — a door, a staircase, a portal — which carries no direction, so there is nowhere on a grid to put them. Their connections are listed on their own pages.

The main landmass — 71 rooms

Wilderness, Set pieces, Marion, Fey Forest, Jasper, Outlying areas, Boss lairs, Tos, Cor Noth, Guild halls.

Landmass 2 — 21 rooms

Ko’catan jungle.

Landmass 3 — 12 rooms

Boss lairs, Wilderness, Set pieces.

Landmass 4 — 3 rooms

Wilderness.

Landmass 5 — 3 rooms

Set pieces, Wilderness.

Landmass 6 — 2 rooms

Wilderness, Tos.

Where the world does not lie flat

115 exits cannot be satisfied by a grid. 37 lead to a square something else already occupies — those rooms are drawn nearby instead, with a dashed outline. 78 lead to a room the grid has already placed elsewhere by a different route. Neither is an extraction error: the world genuinely is not a plane, and these are the seams. They are listed so the map above can be trusted everywhere else.
