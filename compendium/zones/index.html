How this was assembled

A room class declares its map file in prRoom, its identity in piRoom_num, its spawn table in plMonsters with weights, the coordinates those spawns appear at in plGenerators, and the people and objects standing in it as Create() calls with row and column. Exits come in two forms: plEdge_Exits for walking off the edge of the map, and plExits for stepping on a particular square. Both name their destination by room id, which is what makes the links on these pages two-way.

kod/object/active/holder/room.kod and every subclass · extracted by tools/extract-zones.mjs · maps decoded from resource/rooms/*.roo by tools/roo.mjs, following clientd3d/bspload.c

Every number on this site is compiled from the Meridian 59 server source in kod/ and the client sprites in run/localclient/resource. Citations are file:line in that tree. Sprites are the game's own bitmaps, decoded from .bgf. Where a page states a formula, it is the formula the server actually runs — not a community estimate.
