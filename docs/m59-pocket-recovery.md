# Cragged pocket departure recovery

Rooms 578 and 598 contain usable fine-coordinate routes that the square planner cannot reliably board from every landing. A confirmed departure deviation must recover toward the actual destination or a reachable rail suffix. It must not substitute the same rejected waypoint when the destination is outside the local search bound.

`tools/m59-pocket-railbake.mjs` builds `substrate/m59-pocket-rails.json` offline. The seven recorded starting poses cover the east and west pockets in 598 and southern approaches in 578. Each rail continues from the actual fine landing through the existing route's suffix to its intended exit anchor. Every wire-coordinate segment is checked with the collision microstep predicate. The artifact carries the map manifest, movement predicate version, room security and dimensions. Runtime planning verifies the starting bridge, current geometry, mandatory avoidance and live blocking bodies before using it; the paced sender checks again before each packet.

The general fallback also considers compatible rail boarding points and proves an onward segment. A search has a bounded clock. Completed candidate refusals survive a resumed order, so a budget limit does not restart the same search indefinitely or masquerade as proof that a route cannot exist. Body movement, including a single fine unit, changed avoidance, room geometry, sector heights or position invalidates the relevant refusal. A repeated refusal still obtains a new position confirmation. Survival policy runs before departure and between its segments, and a cancelled movement owner cannot send another packet.

Body blockage is separate from static geometry. If a proved escape is obstructed, `deviation.departure` includes its first blocked leg and the observed object's handle, name and named coordinates. An object handle is only valid for that room observation. These details are preserved in movement incidents; a nearby-body list at the starting pose alone can miss a blocker further down the escape passage. The controlling harness retains the wait, alternate refuge, clear-path and operational decisions.

Passage admission retains its cross-process reservation and confirmed-exit rules. It additionally recognizes two published fine necks in 598 that the coarse floor cells classify as open. Admission there is deliberately conservative: measured centre clearance of at most one square is a traffic reservation criterion, not a claim that two players can never pass. Collision still owns every packet, and an incumbent always has the right to leave. These necks are excluded from newly selected resting locations.

A farming quarry whose approach repeatedly refuses is temporarily cooled using the existing target cooldown. The cooldown is invalidated by changed quarry, player, blocking-body or door inputs, and expires normally. It does not ban the room, label a transient body obstruction permanently unreachable or suppress defensive contact already within melee reach.

Validation consists of the movement suite and captured-pose regressions for all seven escape rails, long-distance destination confirmation, unchanged retries, live blocker changes, provenance, mandatory avoidance, paced takeovers, survival preemption, passage admission and quarry cooldowns. A paired deterministic offline comparison against the prior movement build reaches exit anchors in 7 of 7 candidate cases versus 2 of 7 baseline cases. Those replays use static empty geometry and mock confirmations; they do not establish survival in a native server with moving monsters. They also do not measure elapsed game travel time. Production and the local test servers are not operated by these tests.
