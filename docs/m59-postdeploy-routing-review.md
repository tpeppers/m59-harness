Production observation was read-only. Broker identity matched the production roster and deployed checkout. This verifies runtime provenance independently of mutable checkout HEAD.

The first sample at 2026-10-14 01:21 UTC contained 45 distinct incidents from that epoch, beginning at 2026-10-13 23:46 UTC: eight movement deviations, one exhausted region exit, four non-arrivals, three fleet-script failures, 24 cancellations/preemptions, and five equipment stalls. Repeats are separate from these record counts. The deviations reduce to five distinct confirmed poses in Ukgoth and the Outskirts of Tos. The Cragged region refusal concerns the Qor entrance. Current characters had subsequently left those travel incidents; equipment waits and short farming stalls were not classified as pathing failures.

The corrections address four mechanisms:

- Declared running jumps now use the existing stock timed gravity simulation in the shared step primitive. Ukgoth's declared r36c16 to r38c10 jump was using a static fall trace, which predicted the gully and correctly triggered the deviation guard. From captured KOD pose x=1072, y=2304, timed physics instead validates the intended shelf landing. Flight duration, vigor gating, body collision and paced ownership checks remain active. A blocker entering during pacing refuses the jump rather than clipping its landing into the gully. Ordinary planner falls retain their static contract and deviation guard.
- A confirmed, reachable rail bend can take a bounded local body-aware path instead of immediately ending the rail. The search is capped at 100 ms, 3,000 nodes and 32 packets, checks mandatory avoidance, and revalidates each packet against current collision and ownership. The same helper can repair an intermediate waypoint of an ordinary walk; reaching it does not certify the original distant destination.
- A confirmed deviation now consumes an exit-candidate budget slot and permits the next candidate. Exhaustion includes confirmed pose and blocker evidence and reaches travel's existing journey-scoped blocked-hop replanner. Unknown or stale positions, terminal geometry failures, room changes and cancellation still stop the old batch.
- The room router keeps a caller-exhausted hop out of its first expansion on the permissive transit pass. Offline transit uncertainty may relax; a refused approach from the current origin is not thereby offered again. This constraint is supplied by the current journey, with no persistent room ban.
